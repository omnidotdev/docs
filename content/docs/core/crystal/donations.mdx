Not everyone wants a recurring commitment. Donations let supporters send a
one-time contribution of any amount, no subscription required. They are the
simplest way for someone to say thanks.

## How donations work

Once your [payouts](/core/crystal/payouts) are connected, a **Donate** button
appears on your public page and a dedicated donation page lives at:

```
https://crystal.omni.dev/donate/your-handle
```

On that page a supporter:

1. Enters an amount (donations start at a $1 minimum)
2. Optionally adds a short message for you
3. Optionally chooses to donate **anonymously**
4. Pays securely through Stripe Checkout

The funds route straight to your Stripe balance, minus the platform and
processing fees covered in [payments and fees](/core/crystal/payments-fees).

## Anonymous donations

Supporters can choose to keep a donation anonymous. When they do, their name and
avatar are hidden from your public surfaces. The contribution still counts
toward your earnings.

## Seeing your donations

Donation revenue is summarized on the **Earnings** page, where the **Donations**
metric tracks your one-time support over the last 30 days alongside recurring
income and your available balance.

## Nothing to configure

Donations need no setup beyond connecting Stripe. There are no tiers or products
to create: connect your payout account and the Donate button goes live on your
public page.
