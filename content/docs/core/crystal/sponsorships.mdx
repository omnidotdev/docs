title: Sponsorships

---

A sponsorship is the recurring relationship a supporter forms when they
subscribe to one of your [membership tiers](/core/crystal/membership-tiers).
This page covers what happens after someone backs you and how you keep track of
them.

## How a supporter sponsors you

From your public page, a supporter opens the **Support** flow at:

```
https://crystal.omni.dev/sponsor/your-handle
```

There they:

1. Choose a tier
2. Pick **monthly** or **yearly** billing
3. Enter their email for receipts and subscription details
4. Complete payment through Stripe Checkout

The subscription renews automatically, and the supporter appears in your
community right away.

## Managing supporters

The **Supporters** page lists everyone currently backing you, with their tier,
their contribution, and when they joined. You can search by name, and two cards
at the top summarize your total supporters and recent support. Supporters who
chose to stay anonymous are shown without their name or avatar.

Your **Dashboard** also surfaces your most recent supporters and your active
supporter count at a glance.

## Gifting a membership

A supporter can gift a membership to someone else instead of subscribing
themselves. From the Support flow they choose **Gift this membership instead**,
pick the tier and term, and enter the recipient's email plus an optional
message. A gift is a one-time payment that grants the recipient a time-boxed
membership which simply lapses at the end of the term. No recurring subscription
is created for a gift.

## Discord roles

If you have connected Discord for your organization and mapped tiers to roles,
sponsoring a tier can grant the supporter the matching role in your server.
Supporters link their Discord account from their own Profile page so the role
can be applied.

## Fees and payouts

Sponsorship revenue lands in your Stripe balance after the platform and
processing fees described in [payments and fees](/core/crystal/payments-fees),
and is withdrawn like any other earnings from [payouts](/core/crystal/payouts).
