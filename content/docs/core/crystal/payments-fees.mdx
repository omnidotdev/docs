Crystal has two separate money conversations: the **plan** you pay for Crystal
itself, and the **fees** taken from each payment your supporters make. This page
covers both, plus how currency works.

## Your Crystal plan

Your plan sets how much you can build: how many tiers and goals you can publish,
whether you get analytics, custom branding, and API access. Prices are billed to
your organization.

| Plan | Price | Highlights |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Crystal Free** | $0 | 3 membership tiers, 10 posts/month, 2 goals, community support |
| **Crystal Pro** | $15/month or $144/year | 10 membership tiers, unlimited posts, 10 goals, analytics dashboard, API access, priority support |
| **Crystal Team** | $39/month or $384/year | Unlimited tiers, unlimited posts, unlimited goals, custom branding, full analytics, API access, dedicated support |

Manage or upgrade your plan under **Settings** for your organization, where
**Manage Billing** opens the billing portal to update your payment method and
view invoices.

## Fees on each payment

Separately from your plan, a small platform fee is taken from each payment a
supporter makes (memberships, donations, gifts, and digital sales). The rate
depends on your organization type:

| Organization type | Platform fee |
|-------------------|--------------|
| Open-source project | 0% |
| Nonprofit | 0% |
| Creator | 3% |
| Commercial | 5% |

On top of the platform fee, **Stripe charges its own standard processing fee**
for handling the card payment. Both are deducted before the money reaches your
available balance, so what you withdraw is already net of fees. You can see the
net figures in your Stripe Dashboard.

## Currency

Amounts on Crystal are handled in US dollars. Donations start at a $1 minimum.
Payouts settle to your connected bank account in line with your Stripe account's
configuration.

## Taxes

Fees are not taxes. You are the merchant of record for the money your supporters
send, which means any VAT, GST, or sales tax is your responsibility to handle.
Crystal does not calculate, collect, or remit taxes for you. See the tax section
of [payouts](/core/crystal/payouts) for the full detail, including Stripe's
1099-K reporting for qualifying US accounts.
