Crystal turns your audience into a source of income: recurring memberships,
one-time donations, sponsorships, digital sales, and funding goals, with
earnings paid straight to your own bank account through Stripe.

<Callout type="info">
  Crystal is currently in development. Flows and labels may change as it nears
  general availability.
</Callout>

1. **Sign in with your Omni account**
   Crystal uses your Omni account for identity, so signing in (or creating an
   account) takes only a moment. Head to
   [crystal.omni.dev](https://crystal.omni.dev) and sign in.
2. **Create your organization**
   Every creator on Crystal is backed by an organization, which owns your public
   page, tiers, and payouts. If you do not have one yet, use **Create
   Organization** to set one up in your Omni account, then return to Crystal.
3. **Connect Stripe for payouts**
   Before you can accept a single payment, connect and verify a Stripe account
   under **Payouts**. This is the one step everything else depends on, so do it
   first. See [payouts](/core/crystal/payouts).
4. **Polish your public page**
   Set your organization name and description so supporters know who they are
   backing. See [profile](/core/crystal/profile).
5. **Add ways to get supported**
   Create [membership tiers](/core/crystal/membership-tiers) for recurring
   support, set [funding goals](/core/crystal/funding-goals), enable
   [donations](/core/crystal/donations), and list
   [digital products](/core/crystal/digital-sales). Add only what fits your work.
6. **Share and track**
   Share your public page, then watch **Earnings** and **Supporters** to see
   revenue, recurring support, and new backers roll in.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar is your home base once you are signed in:

| Section | What it is for |
|---------|----------------|
| **Dashboard** | Your earnings, supporters, and quick actions at a glance |
| **Profile** | Your account details, subscription, and Discord link |
| **Earnings** | Revenue analytics and payout history |
| **Supporters** | Everyone currently backing you |
| **Tiers** | Create and manage recurring membership tiers |
| **Digital Products** | Files and downloads you sell |
| **Bounties** | Fundable tasks for your project |
| **Grants** | Grant programs you run or take part in |
| **Payouts** | Connect Stripe, view your balance, and withdraw |

When an organization is active, you will also see **Discord** and **Settings**
for that organization near the bottom of the sidebar.
