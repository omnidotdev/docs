title: Your Public Page

---

Every creator on Crystal has a public page where supporters discover your work
and choose how to back you. It lives at:

```
https://crystal.omni.dev/@your-handle
```

Your handle is your organization's slug. From your dashboard you can jump
straight to it with **View Public** on the **Profile** page.

## What supporters see

Your public page pulls together everything you have set up:

- Your **name**, **avatar**, description, and a verified badge when your
  organization is verified
- **Support** and **Donate** buttons (these appear once your
  [payouts](/core/crystal/payouts) are connected; before that, the page shows
  "Coming soon")
- Your published [membership tiers](/core/crystal/membership-tiers), with a
  monthly or yearly billing toggle
- Any [digital products](/core/crystal/digital-sales) you are selling
- A link to your website, when you have set one

## Editing your details

Open **Settings** for your organization (near the bottom of the sidebar when an
organization is active) to update the **Organization Profile**:

- **Organization Name** shown as your display name
- **Description** a short summary of who you are and what supporters help fund

Choose **Save Changes** to publish.

Your avatar, handle, members, and organization ownership are part of your Omni
account rather than Crystal. Use **Manage in Omni** from Settings, or the
**Manage account** link on your Profile page, to change those.

## The Profile page vs. your public page

These are two different things:

- **Profile** (in the sidebar) is your private account view: your account
  details, your current Crystal [plan](/core/crystal/payments-fees), and your
  personal Discord link.
- **Your public page** (`/@your-handle`) is what the world sees.

Use **View Public** on the Profile page to preview exactly what supporters get.
