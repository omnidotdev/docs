---
title: Membership Tiers
description: Create recurring membership tiers supporters can subscribe to
---

Membership tiers are the heart of recurring support on Crystal. Each tier is a
subscription your supporters can join, with its own price and its own list of
benefits. Manage them from **Tiers** in the sidebar.

## Create a tier

1. Open **Tiers** and choose **Create New Tier**.
2. Fill in the tier details:
   - **Name** what supporters see, for example "Supporter" or "Inner Circle"
   - **Description** a short line on what this tier is about
   - **Monthly price** in USD
   - **Benefits** the perks a supporter gets, added as a list
   - **Most popular** highlight one tier to draw attention to it
3. Save. The tier appears on your public page right away.

## Manage your tiers

On the Tiers page you can:

- **Edit** a tier's name, description, price, benefits, or highlight
- **Reorder** tiers with the up and down arrows to control how they stack on
  your public page
- **Delete** a tier you no longer offer

<Callout type="info">
  You cannot delete a tier that still has active supporters. Move those
  supporters to another tier first, so no one's membership is dropped
  unexpectedly.
</Callout>

## How supporters subscribe

On your public page, supporters pick a tier and choose **monthly** or **yearly**
billing. Yearly is billed at twelve times the monthly price unless you offer a
discounted annual rate. Payment runs through Stripe Checkout, and the
subscription renews automatically until the supporter cancels. Everyone backing
you shows up under [sponsorships](/core/crystal/sponsorships).

## How many tiers you can offer

The number of tiers you can publish depends on your Crystal plan: 3 on Free, 10
on Pro, and unlimited on Team. See [payments and fees](/core/crystal/payments-fees)
for the full comparison.

## Discord perks

If you run a Discord community, you can map tiers to server roles so supporters
are granted the right role automatically. Set this up under **Discord** for your
organization. Supporters link their own Discord account from their Profile page.
