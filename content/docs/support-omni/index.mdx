# Support Omni

Omni is 100% open-source and community-driven. Your support helps keep our tools free, independent, and continuously improving.

## Sponsor on Crystal

The best way to support Omni financially is through [Crystal](https://crystal.omni.dev/@omni), our own creator funding platform. Your sponsorship directly funds development, infrastructure, documentation, and community initiatives, and more of every dollar reaches the work than on other platforms.

<a href="https://crystal.omni.dev/@omni" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
  **Sponsor Omni on Crystal**
</a>

For full details on sponsorship tiers and benefits, visit [omni.dev/sponsors](https://omni.dev/sponsors).

You can also sponsor us on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/omnidotdev), though Crystal is our primary platform.

## How Funds Are Used

| Category | Allocation |
|----------|------------|
| Development | 60% |
| Infrastructure | 20% |
| Documentation | 10% |
| Community | 10% |

## Other Ways to Support

- **[Contribute Code](/community/contributing)**: Help build Omni's open-source tools
- **[Join Discord](https://discord.gg/omnidotdev)**: Participate in the community
- **[Report Issues](https://github.com/omnidotdev)**: Help us improve by reporting bugs
- **[Spread the Word](https://x.com/omnidotdev)**: Share Omni with others
