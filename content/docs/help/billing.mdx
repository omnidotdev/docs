Plans, invoices, and credits for every Omni product live in one place: **Billing** at [account.omni.dev/billing](https://account.omni.dev/billing). Billing belongs to a [workspace](/help/workspaces), so the page shows one workspace at a time.

## Choosing a workspace

Use the workspace switcher at the top of the page. It lists only workspaces you belong to, with your role in each. Your **personal workspace** covers what you pay for yourself.

The page remembers the workspace you last chose on that device. A link from a product (for example **Manage billing** or **Buy credits**) opens the workspace that product is billing.

<Callout type="info">
  Only **owners** and **admins** can see and change a workspace's billing. A **member** sees who manages it instead; ask an owner to change the plan, update payment details, or send you an invoice.
</Callout>

## What the page shows

- **Payment method**: the card or account on file. Select **Update payment method** to change it in Stripe, the payment processor.
- **Omni credits**: your prepaid credit balance. Select **Buy credits** to add more.
- **Account credit**: shown only when the workspace has some (see below).
- **Products**: every Omni product the workspace has a plan or usage for, with its plan, status, and renewal date.
- **Invoices**: every invoice for the workspace, across all products, newest first. Select **View** (or **Pay** for an unpaid one) to open the invoice, or **PDF** to download it. Select **Load more invoices** to see older ones.

## Changing or canceling a plan

**1. Select a product** in the Products list. Its page shows the plan, status, and usage against your plan's limits.

**2. Select Change plan** to pick a different plan in Stripe.

**3. To cancel, select Cancel plan** and confirm. Stripe asks you to confirm once more. The plan keeps working until the end of the current billing period, and you can renew it before then.

## Account credit and Omni credits

These are two different balances:

| | Account credit | Omni credits |
|-|----------------|--------------|
| What it is | Money on your workspace's billing account, for example from a refund or an adjustment | Prepaid credits you buy |
| How it is used | Applied automatically to your next invoice | Spent on AI features in any Omni product |
| Where you see it | Billing, only when there is a balance, and on a product's billing page | Billing, and in products that use AI |

A product's own billing page can also show your account credit ("Account credit: $10.00, applied to your next invoice") with an **Invoices** link back to this page.
