Your Omni account is the key to everything you do across the ecosystem, so it is worth protecting. This page explains **two-factor authentication (2FA)**: what it is, how to turn it on, and what to do if you lose access to your device.

## What is two-factor authentication?

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in. After you enter your password, you also enter a short code from an app on your phone. The code changes every 30 seconds, so even if someone learns your password, they cannot sign in without your phone.

<Callout type="info">
  We strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication. It is one of the single most effective things you can do to keep your account safe.
</Callout>

## Before you start

You will need an **authenticator app** on your phone. These are free apps that generate the rotating six-digit codes. Most phones have one available, and any standards-based (TOTP) authenticator app will work with Omni.

## Turning on two-factor authentication

**1. Open your account settings** and go to the **Security** section.

**2. Select Enable 2FA** and confirm your password. This proves it is really you before any change is made.

**3. Scan the QR code** with your authenticator app. The app will add Omni and start showing a six-digit code.

**4. Enter the six-digit code** from your app and select **Verify & activate**.

That's it. Two-factor authentication is now on, and you will be asked for a code the next time you sign in.

<Callout type="info">
  If your phone cannot scan the QR code, you can add the account manually using the setup key shown just below the code.
</Callout>

## Signing in with two-factor authentication

The next time you sign in, you will enter your password as usual, then be asked for the current six-digit code from your authenticator app. Open the app, read the code for your Omni account, and enter it to finish signing in.

## Turning off two-factor authentication

If you need to turn two-factor authentication off:

**1. Open your account settings** and go to the **Security** section.

**2. Select Disable 2FA** and confirm your password.

Your account will then be protected by your password alone. You can turn two-factor authentication back on at any time.

## If you lose access to your device

If you lose the phone with your authenticator app, or you no longer have the codes, you can still recover your account.

<Callout type="warn">
  Do not delete the Omni entry from your authenticator app until you have finished setting up a new one, or you may lock yourself out.
</Callout>

Reach out to [support](/help/support) and our team will help you verify your identity and regain access. If your account is managed by an organization, an administrator can also reset your two-factor authentication for you.
