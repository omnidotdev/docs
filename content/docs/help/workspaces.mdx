Your Omni account can belong to more than one **workspace**, a shared space for a company, project, or group. This page explains how to create workspaces, invite people, give them the right roles, and group them into teams, all from [account.omni.dev](https://account.omni.dev).

## Personal and shared workspaces

Everything you access in Omni lives under a **workspace**, addressed by an `@handle` (for example `@acme`). There are two kinds:

- **Your personal workspace**: created for you automatically. It is just you, so there is nothing to manage.
- **A shared workspace**: one you create for a team. You can belong to many, each with its own members, roles, and teams.

## Creating a workspace

**1. Open [account.omni.dev](https://account.omni.dev)** and go to **Workspaces**.

**2. Select New workspace.** Enter a name and a handle. The handle is checked as you type, so you will know right away if it is available. It becomes your `@handle` across every Omni product, so pick something recognizable.

**3. Select Create.** You are the owner of the new workspace and can start inviting people.

## Inviting members

Open a workspace, then use **Invite a member**:

**1. Enter their email address** and choose a role (see roles below).

**2. Select Invite.** They receive an email with a link to join.

Invitations you have sent appear under **Pending invitations** until they are accepted. An invitation expires after seven days; an expired one is labelled **Expired** and offers a **Resend** button that issues a fresh link.

## Roles

Every member has one of three roles, which control what they can do:

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including editing the workspace, managing all members, and deleting the workspace. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove members, change member roles, and manage teams. Cannot delete the workspace or manage owners. |
| **Member** | Belong to the workspace and its teams, with no management access. |

<Callout type="info">
  Only an owner can grant or change the **owner** role. A workspace always keeps at least one owner, so the last owner cannot be removed or demoted.
</Callout>

To change someone's role, open the workspace and pick a new role next to their name. To remove someone, select **Remove** next to their name and confirm. They lose access but stay in any other workspaces they belong to, and you can invite them back later.

<Callout type="info">
  When a member list gets long, use the search box to find someone by name or email, filter by role, or change the sort order. Your own row is marked **(you)**.
</Callout>

## Teams

Within a workspace you can create **teams**, smaller groups used to organize members and their access (for example an "Engineering" team). Teams live inside a workspace; they are not separate workspaces.

- **Create a team**: select **New team** and give it a name.
- **Rename or delete a team**: use the controls next to the team.
- **Manage a team's members**: select **Members** on a team to expand it, then add any workspace member to the team or remove them from it.

Removing someone from a team does not remove them from the workspace; it only changes that team's membership.

## Editing a workspace

Owners and admins can select **Edit** on a workspace to update its **name**, **handle**, **logo**, and **description**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Changing the handle updates the workspace's `@handle` everywhere it is used, so existing links that reference the old handle may need updating.
</Callout>

## Leaving or deleting a workspace

- **Leaving**: if you are not the sole owner, select **Leave workspace** and confirm. You lose access, and an owner can invite you back later.
- **Deleting**: an owner can delete a workspace from the danger zone. Because this removes it for **every** member, you are asked to type the workspace's name to confirm, and it stays restorable for a grace period before it is permanently removed.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a workspace affects everyone in it. Only delete a workspace you are sure is no longer needed.
</Callout>

Your personal workspace cannot be deleted.
