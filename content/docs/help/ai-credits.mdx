**AI credits** pay for the AI features in Omni products: generating and editing a site in Keystone, managed generation in Facet, the hosted assistant in Beacon, and cloud generation in Creator. Each AI request spends credits based on the model and how much work it does. One credit is $0.01 of AI usage at retail rates.

AI credits pay only for AI. They are separate from [account credit](#ai-credits-and-account-credit), which is money applied to your invoices.

## One balance per workspace

AI credits belong to a [workspace](/help/workspaces), not to a single product. Each workspace has one balance, shared across every Omni product it uses, so credits that come with a Keystone plan can also pay for Facet or Beacon in the same workspace.

Products show the balance of the workspace they bill, for example **1,250 AI credits, shared across Omni products**. Your **personal workspace** has its own balance, separate from any team workspace you belong to.

## Included credits

Many plans include a monthly allowance of AI credits. Allowances from different products add to the same balance: a workspace on Keystone Team that also uses Beacon Free receives both allowances each month.

| Product | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keystone | 150 | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| Beacon | 50 | 300 | 1,000 |
| Creator | 100 | | |
| Facet | None | 2,000 | |

Each product's plans page lists its current allowance.

### When included credits arrive

- **Free plans**: at the start of each calendar month (UTC), or the first time the workspace uses the product that month
- **Paid subscriptions**: at the start of each billing period, once its invoice is paid
- **Plans granted by Omni** (for example a complimentary plan or a bundle): at the start of each calendar month (UTC), and right away when the plan is granted

A free trial of a paid plan does not include the paid allowance; it starts with the first paid billing period. If a workspace moves from a granted plan to a paid subscription, the subscription's allowance replaces what was left of the previous one, so a workspace never holds two allowances for the same product at once.

### Unused credits

Included credits do not roll over. When a period ends, whatever is left of that period's allowance expires and the next period's allowance takes its place. Credits you already spent are never taken back.

Credits you buy do not expire.

## Buying credits

Credits are sold in one place, your Omni account:

**1. Open [Billing](https://account.omni.dev/billing)** and choose the workspace in the switcher. A **Buy credits** link in a product opens Billing with the workspace that product bills already selected.

**2. Select a pack** under **Buy AI credits**, then pay in Stripe Checkout.

**3. Wait a moment.** The credits join the workspace's balance within a minute, and every product that workspace uses can spend them.

### Who can buy

In a team workspace, only **owners** and **admins** can buy credits. A **member** can spend the workspace's credits but cannot buy more; ask an owner or admin. In your personal workspace, you can always buy.

## Running out

When the balance reaches zero, AI features pause. The product shows an out-of-credits message, such as **You're out of AI credits. Buy more credits to keep generating.**, with a **Buy credits** link. Omni never charges overage for AI: nothing is billed until you buy more credits, and AI features resume as soon as credits arrive, either from a purchase or from the next monthly allowance.

A request that is already running when the balance runs out still finishes, so the balance can end slightly below zero. The next purchase or allowance covers that first.

## AI credits and account credit

These are two different balances, and neither turns into the other:

| | AI credits | Account credit |
|-|------------|----------------|
| What it is | Credits for AI usage, from your plan's allowance or a credit pack | Money on your workspace's billing account, for example from a refund or an adjustment |
| How it is used | Spent on AI features in any Omni product | Applied automatically to your next invoice |
| Where you see it | [Billing](/help/billing), and in products that use AI | [Billing](/help/billing), only when there is a balance, and on a product's billing page |
