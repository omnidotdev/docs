Your Omni account can belong to more than one **organization**, a shared space for a company, project, or group. This page explains how to create organizations, invite people, give them the right roles, and group them into teams, all from [account.omni.dev](https://account.omni.dev).

## Organizations and workspaces

Everything you access in Omni lives under a **workspace**, addressed by an `@handle` (for example `@acme`). There are two kinds:

- **Your personal workspace**: created for you automatically. It is just you, so there is nothing to manage.
- **An organization**: a shared workspace you create for a team. You can belong to many organizations, each with its own members, roles, and teams.

<Callout type="info">
  An organization and its workspace are the same thing, seen from two angles: "organization" when you manage its people and settings here, "workspace" when you work inside a product. One `@handle` is always one organization.
</Callout>

## Creating an organization

**1. Open [account.omni.dev](https://account.omni.dev)** and go to **Organizations**.

**2. Select New organization.** Enter a name and a handle. The handle is checked as you type, so you will know right away if it is available. It becomes your `@handle` across every Omni product, so pick something recognizable.

**3. Select Create.** You are the owner of the new organization and can start inviting people.

## Inviting members

Open an organization, then use **Invite a member**:

**1. Enter their email address** and choose a role (see roles below).

**2. Select Invite.** They receive an email with a link to join.

Invitations you have sent appear under **Pending invitations** until they are accepted. An invitation expires after seven days; an expired one is labelled **Expired** and offers a **Resend** button that issues a fresh link.

## Roles

Every member has one of three roles, which control what they can do:

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including editing the organization, managing all members, and deleting the organization. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove members, change member roles, and manage teams. Cannot delete the organization or manage owners. |
| **Member** | Belong to the organization and its teams, with no management access. |

<Callout type="info">
  Only an owner can grant or change the **owner** role. An organization always keeps at least one owner, so the last owner cannot be removed or demoted.
</Callout>

To change someone's role, open the organization and pick a new role next to their name. To remove someone, select **Remove** next to their name and confirm. They lose access but stay in any other organizations they belong to, and you can invite them back later.

<Callout type="info">
  When a member list gets long, use the search box to find someone by name or email, filter by role, or change the sort order. Your own row is marked **(you)**.
</Callout>

## Teams

Within an organization you can create **teams**, smaller groups used to organize members and their access (for example an "Engineering" team). Teams live inside an organization; they are not separate workspaces.

- **Create a team**: select **New team** and give it a name.
- **Rename or delete a team**: use the controls next to the team.
- **Manage a team's members**: select **Members** on a team to expand it, then add any organization member to the team or remove them from it.

Removing someone from a team does not remove them from the organization; it only changes that team's membership.

## Editing an organization

Owners and admins can select **Edit** on an organization to update its **name**, **handle**, **logo**, and **description**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Changing the handle updates the organization's `@handle` everywhere it is used, so existing links that reference the old handle may need updating.
</Callout>

## Leaving or deleting an organization

- **Leaving**: if you are not the sole owner, select **Leave organization** and confirm. You lose access, and an owner can invite you back later.
- **Deleting**: an owner can delete an organization from the danger zone. Because this removes it for **every** member and cannot be undone, you are asked to type the organization's name to confirm.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting an organization is permanent and affects everyone in it. Only delete an organization you are sure is no longer needed.
</Callout>

Your personal workspace cannot be deleted.
