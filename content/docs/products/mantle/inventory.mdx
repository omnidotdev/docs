---
title: Inventory
description: Track products, stock levels, low-stock thresholds, and stock adjustments
---

**Inventory** keeps a list of the products you sell, how many are in stock, and
a history of every change you record.

## Add a product

Select **New product**. The form has three sections:

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Details | Name, SKU, description, unit price |
| Stock | Current quantity, low-stock threshold |
| Status | Active |

SKUs are unique within a workspace. Turn off **Active** for products you no
longer sell; they stay in Inventory under the **Inactive** tab instead of being
deleted.

## The inventory list

The list shows each product with its SKU, unit price, stock, and status.
Search by name or SKU, and filter with the tabs, each with a count:

- **All**
- **Low stock**: at or below the product's low-stock threshold
- **Out of stock**: zero or fewer in stock
- **Inactive**: products marked as not active

The page header sums up how many products are low or out of stock. Each row has
a menu with **View**, **Edit**, and **Delete**.

## Stock levels and low stock

A product's stock level comes from its current quantity:

- **Out**: the quantity is zero or less
- **Low**: the quantity is at or below the low-stock threshold
- No badge: anything above the threshold, or any positive quantity when no
  threshold is set

Low and out badges appear in the list and on the product's page. Active products
at or below their threshold also appear under **Needs attention** on the
[dashboard](/products/mantle/dashboard).

## Stock adjustments

A product's page shows its quantity in stock, unit price, and low-stock
threshold, followed by its **Adjustment history**. Record an adjustment each
time stock changes. The adjustment updates the **In stock** count, and the
history explains how the count got where it is:

1. Select **Adjust stock** (or **Add adjustment** above the history).
2. Enter the **Quantity change**: a positive number for stock added (`10`) or a
   negative number for stock removed (`-5`).
3. Enter a **Reason**, such as Restock, Sold, or Damaged.
4. Save the adjustment.

An adjustment cannot be edited after it is recorded. To correct a mistake,
record another adjustment for the difference. If an adjustment is deleted, its
change to the stock count is reversed.

## Delete a product

Delete a product from its row menu. Mantle asks for confirmation, and deleting
removes the product and its adjustment history. This cannot be undone. To keep
a product's history, mark it inactive instead.
