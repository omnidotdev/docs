---
title: Quotes and contracts
description: Create quotes, price line items, and print a quote or a signed service agreement
---

**Quotes & Contracts** prices out work for a client. Every quote can be shown,
printed, or saved as PDF in two forms: the **quote** itself, and a **contract**
(a service agreement built from the same details, with a signature block).

## Create a quote

Select **New quote**. On a computer, the form sits beside a live preview. On
narrower screens, switch between **Form** and **Preview** at the top. A quote
needs a title before it can be saved.

The form has these sections:

- **Theme**: the accent color shared by all documents in the workspace. See
  [Accent color](/products/mantle/workspace-settings#accent-color).
- **Quote details**: title (required), description, quote number, and status.
  The number is generated when the editor opens. Select **Seq** or **Rand** next
  to it to generate a new sequential or random number instead.
- **Client**: the company, contact, client name, and client email. Use the
  populate button in the section header to pick a company and contact from
  [People](/products/mantle/people) and fill in the client details in one step.
- **Project details**: project type (web, mobile, desktop, API or backend, or
  other), timeline (such as "3-6 months"), valid until, and a label for the
  additional items section (defaults to "Additional deliverables").
- **Line items**: see below.
- **Financials**: tax rate, currency, and the calculated subtotal, tax amount,
  and total.
- **Terms and notes**: payment terms, additional terms, and notes.

Select **Save**. A new quote opens in the editor after its first save, and the
preview and print buttons stay available while you edit.

## Line items

Each line item has a description, an optional **Detail** line shown under it, a
quantity, a unit price, and a pricing type. The amount is the quantity times the
unit price. Select **Add line item** to add more, drag items to reorder them, and
use the trash button to remove one. The first item is labeled **Base development
cost**; later items appear under the additional items label.

### Pricing types

| Pricing type | Use it for | Extra fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed** | A set price for described work | None |
| **Hourly** | Work billed by the hour | Estimated hours, hourly rate, weekly cap, monthly cap, max cap (hours), minimum billing (minutes) |
| **Royalty** | A revenue share | Royalty percentage, timeframe (months, years, or perpetual), revenue threshold, buyout amount |
| **Complimentary** | Work included at no charge | None; the price is set to zero |
| **Included** | Work covered by a retainer | None; the price is set to zero |

Changing an item's pricing type clears the previous type's fields and fills in
starting values (for example, an hourly item starts at a weekly cap of 10 hours
and a monthly cap of 40).

### Deliverables and acceptance criteria

Each line item carries a list of **deliverables** and **acceptance criteria**,
pre-filled for its pricing type. Edit, add, or remove entries. They print in the
scope section of the contract.

## Terms

New quotes start with a set of standard terms (validity, payment terms,
timeline, external costs, maintenance, hour caps, and scope) that you can edit
freely. Two placeholders are filled in when the quote is shown:

- `{companyName}` becomes the company name from your
  [workspace profile](/products/mantle/workspace-settings#workspace-profile).
- `{timeRange}` becomes the quote's timeline.

Add more clauses under **Additional terms**. The printed quote notes that it is
not legally binding and that a separate contract follows if the client accepts.

## Quote statuses

Set the status in **Quote details**: **Draft**, **Sent**, **Accepted**,
**Declined**, **Expired**, or **Converted**. Update the status as the deal
moves along; Mantle sets **Converted** itself when you create an invoice from
the quote. The quotes list has a tab for each status, with counts.

The [dashboard](/products/mantle/dashboard) counts **Sent** quotes as open and
lists sent quotes whose validity ends within the next seven days.

## Create an invoice from a quote

On a **Sent** or **Accepted** quote, select **Create invoice**. Mantle creates a
draft invoice for the same client, with the quote's currency, tax rate, and
notes, opens it for editing, and marks the quote **Converted**. Each line item
carries over by pricing type:

- **Fixed**: the quantity and unit price as quoted
- **Hourly**: the estimated hours at the hourly rate (zero hours when there is
  no estimate, ready to fill in)
- **Complimentary** and **Included**: listed at no charge
- **Royalty**: left off, since it depends on future revenue; Mantle says how
  many were skipped so you can add them when the amount is known

The invoice shows **Created from** with a link back to the quote, and the
quote shows **View invoice** instead of **Create invoice**. A quote can be
invoiced once. Deleting an invoice created from a quote returns the quote to
**Accepted**, so it can be invoiced again.

## The contract view

Open a quote and switch the **Document view** from **Quote** to **Contract**.
The contract is a service agreement generated from the quote:

- The parties: your business (from the workspace profile) and the client
- Scope: project, type, description, and each item's deliverables and
  acceptance criteria
- Pricing, grouped into fixed price, hourly rate, royalty, complimentary, and
  retainer items
- Terms, including the timeline
- Standard legal sections, such as liability, compliance, termination, and
  governing law (which names your registration state when it is set)
- Your additional terms
- A signature block with name, title, signature, and date for each party

Mantle does not collect signatures electronically. Print or save the contract,
then sign it on paper or with the signing tool you already use.

## Print or save as PDF

On a quote's page, select **Print or save PDF** (**Save PDF** in the bottom
action bar on a phone). The browser's print dialog opens with the document that
is currently shown, quote or contract. Choose **Save as PDF** as the destination
to get a file. The suggested file name is the document type and number, such as
`contract_QT-0042`. The editor's **Print** button does the same from the
preview.

## Duplicate and delete

From a quote's row menu, select **Duplicate** to start a new draft quote with
the same details and line items and a fresh number. Select **Delete** to remove
a quote; Mantle asks for confirmation, and deleting cannot be undone.
