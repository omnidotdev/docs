## FAQ

### Does Mantle send quotes and invoices to clients?

No. Print a quote, contract, or invoice, or save it as PDF from the browser, and
send the file yourself. See
[printing quotes](/products/mantle/quotes-and-contracts#print-or-save-as-pdf) and
[printing invoices](/products/mantle/invoices#print-or-save-as-pdf).

### Can clients sign contracts in Mantle?

No. The contract view prints a signature block for both parties. Sign the
printed or saved contract on paper or with your own signing tool.

### Can clients pay invoices online?

Mantle records payments but does not process them. An invoice can carry a
payment link that you copy and share; see
[Payment links](/products/mantle/invoices#payment-links).

### Does accepting a quote create an invoice?

No. Set the quote's status by hand, then create the invoice. To reuse a past
invoice's client and line items, **Duplicate** it.

### Why can't I set an invoice to Paid?

**Paid** is set automatically once recorded payments (plus any applied workspace
credit) cover the total. Record the payment in the invoice's **Payments**
section. See [Statuses](/products/mantle/invoices#statuses).

### What happens when a client overpays?

The excess becomes workspace credit in the invoice's currency, which you can
apply to another invoice. See
[Workspace credit](/products/mantle/invoices#workspace-credit).

### Can I use Mantle in more than one workspace?

Yes. Switch workspaces with the switcher at the top of the sidebar. Each
workspace has its own records, profile, and numbering, and its handle is part of
the address, so links always open the right workspace.

### What happens to my data if a workspace is deleted?

When a workspace is deleted from your Omni account, Mantle deletes everything it
keeps for that workspace: people, quotes, invoices and their payments, credit,
products and their history, numbering, and settings. Export your companies and
contacts first if you want to keep them. See
[Import and export](/products/mantle/people#import-and-export).

### Is Mantle open source?

Yes, under Apache-2.0. See [Self-hosting](/products/mantle/self-hosting).

## Troubleshooting

### After signing in, Mantle shows the home page instead of a workspace

Your account does not belong to a workspace yet. Create one in your Omni
account, then return to Mantle.

### A link opens a different workspace

A link to a workspace you are not a member of opens your default workspace
instead. Ask a member of that workspace to invite you.

### Save is disabled on a quote

A quote needs a title before it can be saved. Fill in **Title** in **Quote
details**.

### Save profile is disabled in Settings

The company name, contact name, and email are required. Fill in all three.

### My logo does not appear on documents

Check that the **Logo URL** in Settings opens the image directly in a browser.
Settings shows "This image could not be loaded" when the link does not work.

### The contract does not name a governing state

Add your **Registration state** in
[Settings](/products/mantle/workspace-settings#workspace-profile).

### An import fails

Use a `.json` file containing a list of records, or a `.csv` file with a header
row using the column names in
[Import and export](/products/mantle/people#import-and-export). When a CSV row
cannot be read, the message names the row near the problem.

### The stock count did not change after an adjustment

Adjustments are recorded in the history only. Edit the product and update its
**Current quantity**. See
[Stock adjustments](/products/mantle/inventory#stock-adjustments).

### Creating a record fails without a clear reason

The workspace may have reached a plan limit. See
[Plans](/products/mantle/plans).
