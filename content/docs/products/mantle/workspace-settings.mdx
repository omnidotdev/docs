title: Workspace settings
description: Business details, branding, your plan, custom fields, and how quotes and invoices are numbered

Open **Settings** from the bottom of the sidebar (or from the
[command palette](/products/mantle/keyboard-shortcuts)). Settings has three
parts: the workspace profile, your plan, and document numbering. All of them
apply to the whole workspace.

Only workspace owners and admins can change workspace settings, including the
profile and document numbering. Other members see them read-only.

## Workspace profile

The workspace profile describes your business. Mantle prints it as the sender on
every quote, contract, and invoice, so fill it in before sending your first
document.

The profile is saved on the workspace and shared by every member, on every
device. If you saved a profile in this browser before profiles moved to the
workspace, Mantle uploads it once, automatically, the first time an owner or
admin opens the workspace.

| Group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Company name (required), website, logo URL, registration state |
| Primary contact | Contact name (required), title, email (required), phone |
| Address | Address lines 1 and 2, city, state, postal code, country |

Select **Save profile** to save. The button stays disabled until the company
name, contact name, and email are filled in.

- **Logo URL**: a link to an image of your logo. A preview appears under the
  field once the image loads. Documents show your logo in their header, or the
  default logo when none is set.
- **Registration state**: the state or jurisdiction your business is registered
  in. The contract view names it in the governing law clause. When it is empty,
  the contract refers to the jurisdiction where your business is registered
  without naming it.
- **Phone**: phone numbers are stored in international format, using the
  country you enter to read a local number.

The **From** section of the invoice editor edits the same profile and saves it
after a short pause, so a change made while writing an invoice also updates
Settings.

## Accent color

Quotes, contracts, and invoices share one accent color, used for the document
header and highlights. Pick a preset or a custom color in the **Theme** section
of the quote editor, or in **Invoice theme** in the invoice editor. The color is
saved with the workspace profile, so every document in the workspace uses it.

## Plan

The **Plan** card shows the workspace's plan and how much of it is in use:
contacts, and invoices created this month, each as a count against the plan's
limit (or "unlimited"). Owners and admins see **Upgrade**, or **Manage billing**
on a paid plan, which opens billing in your Omni account. Other members see a
note that an owner or admin can upgrade. See [Plans](/products/mantle/plans).

If billing is briefly unreachable, the card says so. The workspace's last known
plan applies until billing is back (or no limits, if none is known), and your
records stay available either way.

## Custom fields

Owners and admins add fields to contacts and companies here, such as a
qualification select or a budget number. See
[custom fields](/products/mantle/people#custom-fields).

## Document numbering

Document numbering sets the format of new quote and invoice numbers. Choose
**Quotes** or **Invoices** at the top of the card, adjust the format, and save.
A live **Preview** shows what the next number looks like.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Company prefix | Optional text at the very start, such as `ACME` |
| Prefix | The document prefix. Defaults to `QT` for quotes and `INV` for invoices |
| Separator | Dash (`-`), slash (`/`), or none |
| Date segment | None, `YYYY`, `YYMM`, or `YYYYMM`, from the date the number is generated |
| ID strategy | **Sequential**, **Random**, or **Timestamp** |
| Sequential padding | For sequential numbers, how many digits to pad to (`0001`) |
| Random length | For random numbers, how many characters to generate |

The strategies produce:

- **Sequential**: the next number in a running count, such as `INV-0042`.
  Mantle reserves each number when it is generated, so two people creating
  invoices at the same time never get the same one.
- **Random**: a random uppercase code, such as `INV-A7F3BC90`.
- **Timestamp**: the date and time the number was generated, such as
  `INV-20260203-115447`.

A new quote or invoice gets its number when the editor opens. Numbers are unique
within a workspace. To give one client its own invoice sequence, see
[per-client numbering](/products/mantle/invoices#per-client-numbering).
