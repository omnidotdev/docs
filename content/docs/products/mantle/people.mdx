title: People
description: Companies, contacts, tags, memberships, import and export, and the network view
---

**People** holds the companies and contacts you work with. Quotes and invoices
pick their client from here, so a client entered once is reused everywhere.

People has three tabs, each with a count: **Companies**, **Contacts**, and
**Network**.

## Companies

Select **Add company** (or **New company** from the create button on a phone).
Only the name is required.

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Details | Name, website, industry, email, phone |
| Address | Address lines 1 and 2, city, state, postal code, country |
| Notes | Free-form notes |

The companies list shows name, industry, email, and phone. Search matches the
name, industry, email, phone, or website. Select a company to open it.

A company's page also has:

- **Members**: the contacts who belong to the company, each with an optional
  title. Mark one as the **Primary contact**. Removing a member only removes
  their link to the company; the contact stays in People.
- **Invoice numbering**: an optional numbering sequence used for this client's
  invoices. See [per-client numbering](/products/mantle/invoices#per-client-numbering).

## Contacts

Select **Add contact**. First and last name are required.

- **Details**: first name, last name, phone, and **Internal team member**. Turn
  on Internal team member for people inside your own business, so they can be
  told apart from clients and partners.
- **Email addresses**: add as many as needed. Press **Enter** after each one.
- **Tags**: toggle the suggested tags (employee, client, prospect, active,
  inactive, design, engineering, research, leadership, contractor) or type your
  own and press **Enter** or **,** to add it. Tags are saved in lowercase.
- **Notes**: free-form notes.

The contacts list has its own filter for **All**, **Internal**, and
**External** contacts, with counts, and searches by name, email, and phone.

A contact's page also has **Companies**: the companies the contact belongs to,
with a title for each and an optional **Primary company**. Removing a company
there only removes the link; the company stays in People.

## Deleting

Delete a company or contact from its row menu or from its page. Mantle asks for
confirmation, and deleting cannot be undone.

## Import and export

Select **Import or export** on the Companies or Contacts tab. The dialog works
on whichever tab is open.

**Export** downloads every company or contact in the workspace as **JSON** or
**CSV**. In CSV, list fields such as emails and tags are joined with a
semicolon (`;`).

**Import** reads a `.json` or `.csv` file and creates one record per row:

- A JSON file must contain a list of records.
- A CSV file needs a header row. Use these column names:

| Importing | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| Contacts | `firstName`, `lastName`, `emails`, `phone`, `avatarUrl`, `tags`, `internal`, `notes` |
| Companies | `name`, `website`, `industry`, `phone`, `email`, `addressLine1`, `addressLine2`, `city`, `state`, `postalCode`, `country`, `notes` |

Separate multiple emails or tags in one cell with a semicolon, and write
`true` in `internal` for internal team members. A file exported from Mantle can
be imported again as is. Import always adds new records; it does not update or
merge existing ones, so importing the same file twice creates duplicates.

## Network view

The **Network** tab draws your contacts as a graph. Each contact is a dot, and
two contacts are connected when they share at least one tag. A contact's color
comes from its first tag with a known color, shown in the legend (employee,
client, prospect, active, contractor, leadership, and other). Drag to move
around and scroll to zoom.

Contacts without tags appear as unconnected dots. Tag contacts to see how they
connect.
