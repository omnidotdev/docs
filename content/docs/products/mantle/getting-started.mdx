This guide takes you from signing in to your first contact, quote, and invoice.

1. **Sign in with your Omni account**
   Open [mantle.omni.dev](https://mantle.omni.dev) and select **Sign in** (or
   **Get started free**). Mantle signs you in with your Omni account, so there is
   nothing to install and no separate password.
2. **Open a workspace**
   After sign-in, Mantle opens the workspace you used last, or your first
   workspace on a first visit. The workspace handle is part of the address
   (`mantle.omni.dev/@your-workspace`). To change workspace, use the workspace
   switcher at the top of the sidebar. Workspaces are created and managed in your
   Omni account, and the switcher links there.
3. **Fill in your workspace profile**
   Open **Settings** from the bottom of the sidebar and enter your business name,
   contact name, and email (the three required fields), plus your address and
   logo. These details print on every quote, contract, and invoice, and are
   shared by everyone in the workspace. Only workspace owners and admins can
   change them. See
   [Workspace settings](/products/mantle/workspace-settings).
4. **Add a contact or company**
   A new workspace shows a **Get started** card on the dashboard. Select **Add a
   contact**, or open **People** and add a company. See
   [People](/products/mantle/people).
5. **Create a quote**
   Open **Quotes & Contracts** and select **New quote**. Give it a title, pick
   the client, add line items, and save. See
   [Quotes and contracts](/products/mantle/quotes-and-contracts).
6. **Create an invoice**
   Open **Invoices** and select **New invoice**. Pick the client, add line
   items, set the issue and due dates, and save. Record payments as they arrive.
   See [Invoices](/products/mantle/invoices).

## Find your way around

On a computer, the sidebar lists the dashboard and each module, with **Settings**,
the theme toggle, and **Sign out** at the bottom. The top bar shows where you are
and a **Search or jump to** button that opens the
[command palette](/products/mantle/keyboard-shortcuts).

On a phone, Mantle uses a phone-first layout:

- A **tab bar** at the bottom switches between **Home** (the dashboard),
  **People**, **Invoices**, **Quotes**, and **Inventory**.
- A **create button** (the round **+** above the tab bar) on the dashboard and
  on each module's list opens a menu of new items: invoice, quote, contact,
  company, and product. The current module's action is listed first.
- Detail and editor pages move their main actions (such as **Edit**, **Save**,
  and **Save PDF**) to an **action bar** at the bottom of the screen.
- Lists show each record as a card instead of a table row.
- The menu button at the top left opens the sidebar, including the workspace
  switcher and **Settings**.

## Lists, tabs, and search

Every module's list works the same way:

- **Tabs with counts** filter the list (by status for quotes and invoices, by
  stock level for inventory, by view in People). Each tab shows how many
  records it holds, and the counts follow the current search.
- **Search** narrows the list as you type.
- **Column headers** sort the table. Sorting, search, and the selected tab are
  kept in the address, so a link or a refresh keeps the same view.
- Each row has a menu of actions such as **View**, **Edit**, **Duplicate**, and
  **Delete**. Deleting always asks for confirmation first.
