---
title: Invoices
description: Create invoices, record payments, apply workspace credit, and print or save PDFs
---

**Invoices** bills clients and tracks what they owe. Each invoice shows a live,
branded preview and keeps a list of the payments made against it.

## Create an invoice

Select **New invoice**. On a computer, the form sits beside a live preview. On
narrower screens, switch between **Form** and **Preview** at the top.

The form has these sections:

- **From**: your business name, contact name, email, phone, and address. These
  fields edit the
  [workspace profile](/products/mantle/workspace-settings#workspace-profile)
  directly, so they carry over to every document.
- **Invoice theme**: the accent color shared by all documents in the workspace.
- **Invoice details**: invoice number, status, issue date (today by default),
  and due date.
- **Bill to**: the recipient's name and address as free text. Use the populate
  button in the section header to pick a company and contact from
  [People](/products/mantle/people); Mantle links the invoice to that client and
  fills in the bill-to text.
- **Line items**: a description, quantity, and unit price for each line. The
  amount is the quantity times the unit price. Select **Add line item** to add
  more, and drag lines to reorder them.
- **Totals**: tax rate and currency, with the subtotal, tax amount, and total
  calculated for you.
- **Payments**: see below.
- **Notes**: payment terms, a thank-you note, or anything else for the client.

Select **Save**. The totals are recalculated on the server from the line items,
so the saved total always matches the lines.

## Invoice numbers

A new invoice gets its number from the workspace numbering scheme when the
editor opens (see
[Document numbering](/products/mantle/workspace-settings#document-numbering)).
Select the refresh button next to the number to generate the next one. When you
pick a client with the populate button on a new invoice, the number is drawn from
that client's own sequence if it has one. You can also type a number by hand.
Invoice numbers are unique within a workspace.

### Per-client numbering

To give a client its own invoice sequence, open the company in
[People](/products/mantle/people) and find **Invoice numbering**:

1. Turn on **Custom invoice numbering for this client**.
2. Enter a prefix, such as `TDTS`.
3. Choose **Sequential**, **Random**, or **Timestamp**, and check the preview.
4. Select **Save numbering**.

The client's sequence keeps the rest of the workspace format (separator, date
segment, and padding). Turn the switch off to return the client to the
workspace numbering.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Not sent yet |
| **Sent** | Sent to the client |
| **Viewed** | The client has seen it |
| **Partially paid** | Some of the total has been paid |
| **Paid** | Payments (and any applied credit) cover the total |
| **Overdue** | Past due |
| **Void** | Cancelled; no longer owed |

Set the status by hand in **Invoice details**, with one exception: **Paid** is
set automatically once payments cover the total, and cannot be chosen by hand.
Removing a payment so a balance remains moves a paid invoice back to **Sent**.
Mantle does not mark invoices as viewed or overdue on its own, but the
[dashboard](/products/mantle/dashboard) counts any sent invoice past its due
date as overdue.

The totals of a **Paid** or **Void** invoice are frozen: editing its line items
never changes the recorded total.

The invoices list has a tab for each status, with counts, and searches by
invoice number or client.

## Record payments

An invoice can take any number of payments, for example a deposit and a final
payment. In the **Payments** section of the editor, select **Add payment** and
enter:

- **Date** the payment arrived
- **Amount**
- **Reference**, such as a check number or transfer ID
- **Method**, such as check, wire, card, or cash

The section header shows the remaining balance, or **Paid in full**. On the
invoice's page, a balance card shows how much has been paid with a progress
bar, and **Record payment** opens the editor. Payments are also listed on the
page with their method, date, reference, and amount.

## Workspace credit

When payments add up to more than an invoice's total, Mantle turns the excess
into **workspace credit** automatically, in the invoice's currency. Recording
another payment later only adds the new excess, never the same overpayment
twice.

When the workspace has credit in an invoice's currency and the invoice still has
a balance, the invoice's page shows a credit panel with the available credit
and the outstanding balance. Select **Apply credit** and confirm to draw the
credit down against the invoice. Credit applied to an invoice counts toward
paying it, and applying credit cannot be undone.

## Payment links

An invoice can carry a payment link: a URL where the client pays online. When an
invoice has one, its page shows **Copy payment link**. Mantle does not create
payment links itself, and the invoice editor has no field for one; a link is
attached to an invoice through the Mantle API.

## Print or save as PDF

On an invoice's page, select **Print or save PDF** (**Save PDF** in the bottom
action bar on a phone). The browser's print dialog opens with the invoice.
Choose **Save as PDF** as the destination to get a file. The suggested file name
is `invoice_` followed by the invoice number.

The printed invoice includes your logo and accent color, the line items, totals,
and notes. Turn on **Show quantity and unit price** above the preview to print
those columns. Once an invoice has payments, it lists them with the amount paid
and the balance due. A paid invoice shows a **Paid** badge and the date it was
paid in full, so it doubles as a receipt.

## Duplicate and delete

Select **Duplicate** on an invoice's page or in its row menu to start a new
invoice with the same client, bill-to text, line items, tax rate, currency, and
notes. The copy gets a fresh number and dates, starts as a draft, and has no
payments.

Select **Delete invoice** to remove an invoice and its payments. Mantle asks for
confirmation, and deleting cannot be undone.
