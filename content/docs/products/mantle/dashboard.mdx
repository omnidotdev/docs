title: Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page of every workspace (**Home** in the phone tab
bar). It shows where money stands this month and lists what needs action.

## Getting started card

A workspace with no invoices, quotes, contacts, or companies yet shows a **Get started** card
with three first steps: **Add a contact**, **Create a quote**, and **Create an
invoice**. It goes away once the workspace has records.

## Summary cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Outstanding** | The unpaid balance across sent, viewed, and overdue invoices, and how many are open |
| **Overdue** | The unpaid balance of open invoices that are marked overdue or are past their due date, and how many |
| **Paid this month** | Payments received this calendar month, by payment date, with the change from last month |
| **Open quotes** | The total of quotes with the **Sent** status, and how many are awaiting a decision |

Balances subtract payments already recorded. Draft and void invoices are left
out. When a workspace bills in more than one currency, the cards use the most
common one and say so under the cards.

## Invoiced per month

A chart of the amount invoiced in each of the last six months, by issue date.
Drafts and void invoices are not counted.

## Recent invoices

The latest invoices, with client, amount, and status. Select one to open it.

## Needs attention

A short list of things to act on:

- **Overdue invoices**, most overdue first, with the number of days past due
  and the balance
- **Low-stock products**: active products at or below their low-stock threshold
- **Expiring quotes**: sent quotes whose validity ends within the next seven
  days

The card is hidden when nothing needs attention.

## Modules

Links to each module with a count of its records.

The **New quote** and **New invoice** buttons at the top of the dashboard start
a new document. On a phone, use the create button above the tab bar.
