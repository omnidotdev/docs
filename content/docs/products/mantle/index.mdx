**Omni Mantle** is a business operations suite. It brings four modules into one workspace, so a contact you add once can be quoted, contracted, and invoiced without retyping anything.

## Modules

- **People**: companies and contacts, tags, company memberships, import and export, and a network view of how contacts connect
- **Quotes & Contracts**: quotes with fixed, hourly, royalty, complimentary, and included line items, each printable as a quote or as a service agreement with a signature block, and turned into a draft invoice in one click
- **Invoices**: branded invoices with a live preview, payment links, multiple payments per invoice, workspace credit from overpayments, and per-client numbering
- **Inventory**: products with SKUs and prices, stock levels, low-stock thresholds, and a history of stock adjustments

A dashboard ties them together with outstanding and overdue balances, payments received this month, open quotes, and a list of items that need attention.

## How it fits together

- Everything in Mantle belongs to a **workspace** (your Omni workspace). The active workspace is part of every address, for example `mantle.omni.dev/@acme/invoices`, so a link always opens the same workspace.
- Your **workspace profile** (shared by every member: business name, contact details, address, logo, and accent color) fills in the "from" side of every quote, contract, and invoice.
- **Document numbering** is set per workspace for quotes and invoices, and a client can have its own invoice sequence.
- Quotes and invoices are printed, or saved as PDF, from the browser. Mantle does not email documents for you.
