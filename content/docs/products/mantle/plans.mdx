---
title: Plans
description: Where to find Mantle's current plans and pricing
---

Mantle plans belong to a workspace, not to an individual. A workspace's plan
sets limits such as how many contacts it can keep and how many invoices it can
create each month.

Current plans and prices are always shown on the
[Omni pricing page](https://omni.dev/pricing).

When a workspace reaches a limit, Mantle does not save the new record. Nothing
already saved is removed or hidden. Contacts marked as internal team members do
not count toward the contact limit.

## Self-hosting

No plan limits apply to a [self-hosted](/products/mantle/self-hosting)
install.
