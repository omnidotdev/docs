Mantle plans belong to a workspace, not to an individual, and every plan
includes unlimited team members. Prices are on the
[Mantle pricing page](https://mantle.omni.dev/pricing).

| Plan | Contacts | Invoices per month | Also includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Up to 100 | Up to 10 | Unlimited team members, quotes, contracts, and inventory |
| **Pro** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Everything in Free |
| **Team** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Everything in Pro, plus priority support |

See a workspace's plan and usage in
[Settings](/products/mantle/workspace-settings#plan), where owners and admins
can upgrade or manage billing.

## How limits are enforced

- Limits only stop you from creating a new contact or invoice past the cap.
  Reading, editing, and deleting existing records always work, and nothing is
  hidden or removed when a plan changes.
- Every contact counts toward the contact limit, including internal team
  members.
- The invoice limit counts invoices created in the current calendar month.
  Deleting an invoice does not give back its monthly slot.
- If billing is unreachable, the workspace's last known plan applies; with none
  known, nothing is limited.

## Self-hosting

No plan limits apply to a [self-hosted](/products/mantle/self-hosting)
install.
