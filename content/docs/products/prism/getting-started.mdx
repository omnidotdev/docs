---
title: Getting Started
description: Point a product at the collector and watch events roll up
---

1. **Sign In**
   Prism dashboards are gated behind an Omni sign-in. Sign in at [prism.omni.dev](https://prism.omni.dev) to reach your workspace. Prefer a look before you commit? The [public demo](https://prism.omni.dev/demo) runs on sample data.
2. **Instrument a Product**
   Point an app at the collector with a single environment variable and report pageviews through the shared analytics client. See [Instrumenting a Product](/products/prism/instrumenting) for the three-step setup.
3. **Send Events**
   Pageviews report automatically on route change. Track anything else with a named call, for example `prism.track("app_opened", { appId })`.
4. **Read the Dashboard**
   Open the [dashboard](/products/prism/dashboard) to see per-product daily rollups: visitors, pageviews, top pages, and deltas over a range you control.
5. **Upgrade as You Grow**
   The free tier is hard-capped for hobby use. When you need more events, retention, or products, move to a paid [tier](/products/prism/pricing).
