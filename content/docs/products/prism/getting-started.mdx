## Try the demo

The [public demo](https://prism.omni.dev/demo) runs the real dashboard on sample data, with no sign-in.

## Sign in

Sign in at [prism.omni.dev](https://prism.omni.dev) with your Omni account. Prism uses your Omni workspaces: each product belongs to a workspace, and every member of that workspace can read its analytics. Workspaces and their members are managed in the Omni account hub at [account.omni.dev](https://account.omni.dev).

## Create a product

1. Open your workspace and create a product. Give it a name, and optionally the domain it runs on.
2. Copy the product's collector key (`pk_...`) from its install instructions.

Only workspace owners and admins can create, edit, rotate the key of, or delete a product.

## Install the tag

Paste the tag into the `<head>` of every page:

```html
<script defer src="https://api.prism.omni.dev/script.js" data-product="pk_..."></script>
```

It records a pageview on load and on every client-side navigation. For bundled apps, custom events, or server-side reporting, see [Installing](/products/prism/installing).

## See your first pageview

Visit your site from a normal browser tab. The tag skips `localhost` by default, so test against a deployed URL (or add `data-allow-localhost` while you develop).

- **Realtime visitors** on the dashboard counts visitors from the last five minutes, read straight from incoming events.
- Charts and breakdowns refresh every few minutes, as rollups run.
