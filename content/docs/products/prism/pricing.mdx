## How limits are enforced

### Events

Events are counted per workspace per UTC calendar month, across all of its products. Pageviews and custom events both count; bots never do.

- **Free** stops accepting events at the allowance. The collector returns `429` until the next month starts.
- **Pro and Team** keep accepting events up to 1.5 times the allowance before stopping, so a traffic spike does not lose data.

### Products

Creating a product beyond the plan's limit is refused with a prompt to upgrade.

### Retention

Data older than the retention window is purged hourly, and the dashboard and API never show data past it. A product can set a shorter retention of its own; the effective window is the shorter of the plan's and the product's.

### Members

Every plan includes unlimited members, so every member of a workspace can read its analytics. Members are managed in the Omni account hub at [account.omni.dev](https://account.omni.dev).

## Self-hosting

No plan limits apply to a [self-hosted](/products/prism/self-hosting) install.
