---
title: Pricing
description: Prism tiers, from free hobby use to team scale
---

Prism is priced by monthly event volume. The free tier is hard-capped for hobby use; paid tiers raise the event allowance, retention window, and number of products you can track. Live pricing is always shown at [prism.omni.dev/pricing](https://prism.omni.dev/pricing).

## Free

Start measuring, no cost.

- 10,000 events/month
- 1 product
- 30-day data retention
- Cookieless, privacy-first by default
- Pageviews and custom events

## Pro

For growing products. **$9/month** (or $90/year).

- Everything in Free
- 500,000 events/month
- Up to 10 products
- 1-year data retention
- GraphQL read API
- Up to 5 members

## Team

For teams and scale. **$29/month** (or $290/year).

- Everything in Pro
- 5,000,000 events/month
- Unlimited products
- Unlimited data retention
- GraphQL read API
- Unlimited members
- Priority support

## Self-hosting

Prism is Apache-2.0 and self-hostable. Run the full stack yourself with Docker Compose and the tiers above do not apply: see the [overview](/products/prism) for the self-hosting paths.
