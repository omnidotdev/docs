**Omni Prism** is first-party, privacy-first product analytics. It ingests pageviews and custom events over a public, cookieless HTTP collector and materializes them into fast daily rollups you read from a dashboard or a GraphQL API.

Prism counts visitors with a daily-rotating salted hash, never a raw IP or a persistent cookie, so there is nothing to consent to and no cross-site profile to build. Every product in your workspace reports through the shared analytics client in [`@omnidotdev/providers`](https://github.com/omnidotdev/providers), so there is no Prism-specific SDK to install.

## Self-Hosting

Run Prism on your own machine with one command, or spin up the source with live
reload to contribute.
