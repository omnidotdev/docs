**Omni Charm** is a free, open-source, self-hostable all-in-one Discord bot for community management: themed welcome cards, moderation, automod and anti-raid, reaction roles, onboarding, leveling, tickets, and full audit logging, behind a genuinely polished configuration dashboard with per-server theming included and never paywalled.
