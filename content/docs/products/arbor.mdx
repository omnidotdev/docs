**Omni Arbor** is a graph-UI git provider that helps you visualize and model your code repositories, with a focus on multi-repo configurations. Arbor is Apache-2.0 licensed and self-hostable.

<Callout type="warn">
  Arbor is in a closed beta. **[Apply for the closed beta](https://arbor.omni.dev/apply)** to get early access.
</Callout>

## Move a repository to another workspace

To move a repository to another of your workspaces, open its settings and select **Move repository**, then pick the workspace. You need to be an owner or admin of the workspace the repository is in (or own it, for a personal repository) and of the target. The confirm names the target and what changes:

- The repository leaves its current workspace and belongs to the target, whose members get access through it from then on
- You become its owner. Its history, branches, and collaborators stay
- It counts toward the target workspace's plan

Old links keep working: a link or bookmark to the repository's previous page redirects to the same page where it lives now, and its old clone URL keeps working for clone, fetch, and push. A link only redirects for people who may open the repository.
