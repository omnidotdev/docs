Fractal plans pair a flat monthly price with a monthly usage credit that covers
your compute. You only pay more if your usage goes past the credit, billed by the
hour. See [fractal.omni.dev/pricing](https://fractal.omni.dev/pricing) for current
plans and prices.

## Start with a trial

New teams start with a **14-day free trial** of the Pro plan, no card required.
When the trial ends, add a payment method to continue. If you do not, your
services are paused (scaled to zero) and your data is retained for a grace period
so you can pick up where you left off by adding a card.

## How usage-based billing works

Each plan includes a monthly **usage credit** (for example, the credit equals the
plan's flat price on the Hobby and Pro plans). Compute usage draws from that credit
first. Anything past the credit is added to that month's invoice at clear per-hour
rates:

- **Compute (CPU)** is metered per vCPU and prorated to the second.
- **Compute (memory)** is metered per GB and prorated to the second.
- **Bandwidth** is billed per GB of egress past your plan's included transfer.

Storage is included up to each plan's limit and is not billed as overage. Projects,
services, custom domains, and team seats are plan features, not metered usage.

The exact overage rates and each plan's included credit, storage, and bandwidth are
listed on the [pricing page](https://fractal.omni.dev/pricing).

## Keeping costs predictable

- **Scale-to-zero**: idle services sleep and stop billing compute, then wake
  instantly on the next request.
- **Spend limits**: set a monthly cap so usage charges never exceed an amount you
  choose.
- **No surprises**: you only ever pay your flat plan price plus any usage above your
  credit. There are no per-seat compute markups and no minimums beyond the plan price.

## Managing your plan

Open your workspace **Billing** settings to change plans, update payment methods, or
open the billing portal. Upgrades are prorated immediately; downgrades apply a credit
to your next cycle. If you cancel, your data is retained unless you ask us to delete
it, or we warn you before any deletion.

## Running on your own cluster

Prefer to run Fractal on infrastructure you own? **Bring Your Own Cloud** offers a
flat per-cluster price with no per-app compute markup, and is rolling out with design
partners first. See [self-hosting](/products/fractal/self-hosting) or
[apply for early access](https://fractal.omni.dev/early-access).
