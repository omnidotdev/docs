---
title: Custom Domains
description: Serve a service on your own domain with automatic TLS
---

Every exposed service gets a generated `*.fractaldeploy.app` domain automatically.
To serve it on your own domain instead, add a custom domain from the service's
**Settings** tab.

A custom domain is one you own. Fractal addresses (`*.fractaldeploy.app` and other
Fractal-issued hosts) are already served with automatic HTTPS, so the custom domain
field does not accept them.

## Add a domain

1. Open the service and go to **Settings → Networking**.
2. Enter your domain (for example `app.example.com`) and save (or press Enter).
3. [Verify that you own the domain](#verify-ownership): add the TXT record
   Fractal shows at your DNS provider.
4. Once the domain is verified, Fractal shows the **DNS records** that route it.
   Copy each record into your DNS provider.
5. The certificate is issued automatically once the records resolve. The
   **Certificate** badge advances to _Active_, with no downtime.

## Verify ownership

Before a custom domain serves, you prove that you control its DNS. This stops
anyone else from connecting a domain you own to their service.

After you save the domain, the panel shows a TXT record to add:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | `TXT` |
| Name | `_fractal-challenge.` followed by your domain, for example `_fractal-challenge.app.example.com` |
| Value | `fractal-domain-verification=` followed by a code unique to your project and domain |

Copy the exact name and value from the panel. Some DNS providers add your domain
to record names automatically; if yours does, enter only the part of the name
before your domain (for example `_fractal-challenge.app`).

Fractal looks the record up through public DNS automatically, often within
seconds at first and then less frequently. Select **Check now** to look it up
immediately. The panel reports one of:

- **Verify you own this domain**: the record has not been found yet. New DNS
  records can take a few minutes to propagate.
- **Ownership not verified**: a record was found but holds a different value,
  or the domain is already connected to a service in another project. The panel
  says which.
- **Ownership: Verified**: the domain is yours, and setup continues with the DNS
  records that route it.

Until the custom domain is verified and live, your service keeps serving on its
`*.fractaldeploy.app` address, and the panel shows both: the address it is
serving on now and the custom domain still being set up. Once the custom domain
is live, it replaces that address. Changing the domain starts a new
verification for the new one, and removing it brings the `*.fractaldeploy.app`
address back.

The code in the record is tied to your project, so the same domain needs a new
record if you move it to a service in another project. A domain verified for
one project cannot be connected to a service in a different project until it is
removed from the first.

<Callout title="Domains connected before verification">
Custom domains that were already live when ownership verification was
introduced stay verified, with nothing to add.
</Callout>

## Keep the record in place

Leave the TXT record at your DNS provider after the domain is verified. Fractal
looks it up again about once a day. If it goes missing or its value changes:

- The domain stays connected, and the panel shows **Ownership record missing**
  with the record to restore and the date to restore it by.
- The workspace's members get an email with the same date.
- If the record is still missing after a week of daily checks, the domain is
  disconnected and needs to be verified again. A single failed lookup never
  disconnects a live domain.

Adding the record back before the deadline clears the warning. Domains that were
already live when ownership verification was introduced have no record, so they
are not re-checked.

<Callout title="Zero-downtime issuance">
Fractal uses DNS-based certificate validation, so the certificate is issued and
ready before any traffic moves to your domain. Your existing
`*.fractaldeploy.app` URL keeps working until the custom domain is live.
</Callout>

## DNS records

Besides the [ownership TXT record](#verify-ownership), you typically add two
records once the domain is verified:

| Record | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Traffic `CNAME` | Points your domain at Fractal's edge |
| Verification record | Lets the certificate issue for your domain |

The certificate is validated and served for you; the exact record names, types,
and values are shown in the dashboard for your specific domain, each with a copy
button.

## apex and www

Set **www** to `redirect` in the same Networking section to send
`www.example.com` to the apex `example.com` automatically. This is the common
"apex plus www" setup and needs no second custom-domain slot.

## Certificate status

The **Certificate** badge reflects whether your domain actually serves your
service. Fractal checks this by requesting your domain over the public internet,
so the badge reads _Active_ as soon as traffic reaches your service, including
when your domain is proxied through its own Cloudflare zone.

- **Verifying**: waiting for the DNS records to resolve
- **Issuing**: the certificate is being issued
- **Pending**: the certificate is ready but the traffic record is not in place
  yet, or the domain no longer points at Fractal
- **Active**: the domain serves your service
- **Error**: something only you can fix, such as a domain that could not be
  registered because another provider has claimed it

Every state other than _Active_ shows a short message under the badge saying what
the domain is waiting on. If a certificate's validation times out, Fractal
restarts it automatically, so a domain recovers on its own once the DNS records
are correct.

Your existing `*.fractaldeploy.app` URL keeps serving until the custom domain is
live, so there is never a gap while it finishes setting up.

## Plan limits

Custom domains add no per-domain setup on your side beyond the DNS records, so the
only limit is how many your plan includes. If a domain is gated by your plan, the
panel shows an upgrade prompt instead of DNS records, and the service keeps serving
on its generated subdomain. See
[fractal.omni.dev](https://fractal.omni.dev) for current plan limits.
