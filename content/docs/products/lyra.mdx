**Omni Lyra** teaches you to play music, starting with piano and keys. It pairs zero-hardware, tinker-in-the-page lessons with a real-time practice trainer that grades your timing and accuracy against a scrolling track, on web, desktop, and mobile from a single app.

## Key Features

- **Concept Explorer**: Interactive, no-hardware lessons on beats, notes, chords, and song structure
- **Practice Trainer**: Real-time timing and accuracy grading against a scrolling note-highway
- **Play Anywhere**: On-screen keyboard for touch and computer keys, plus MIDI when you plug in a device
- **Guided Onboarding**: A short placement quiz routes you to lessons or drills and sets your starting difficulty
- **Progress and Streaks**: Track skill mastery and keep a daily practice habit

## Getting Started

1. Open [lyra.omni.dev](https://lyra.omni.dev) in your browser, or install the app.
2. Sign in with your Omni account, or continue as a guest to explore the free lessons.
3. Take the short placement quiz so Lyra can tailor your path.
4. Calibrate your device (a quick tap-to-the-beat) for honest timing feedback.
5. Start your first lesson or practice session and build your streak.

## Plans

Lyra is free-first. The concept-explorer lessons and a daily practice quota are free forever. A paid tier unlocks unlimited practice, the full drill and song catalog, the hardware MIDI trainer, and advanced progress analytics.
