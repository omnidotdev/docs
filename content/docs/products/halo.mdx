**Omni Halo** is a modular ecommerce platform with zero-trust architecture and cryptocurrency support. Build your store with exactly the components you need while maintaining enterprise-grade security.

## Key Features

- **Modular Architecture**: Build your store with the exact components you need
- **Zero-Trust Security**: Enhanced security model that verifies every access request
- **Multi-Payment Support**: Accept both traditional fiat and cryptocurrency payments
- **Customizable Workflows**: Tailor the purchase journey to your specific business needs
- **API-First Design**: Easily integrate with other services and platforms

## Use Cases

- **Digital Product Stores**: Streamlined selling of digital goods with automated delivery
- **Subscription Services**: Flexible recurring payment models with various billing options
- **Marketplace Platforms**: Connect buyers and sellers with secure transaction processing
- **International Commerce**: Reduce friction for cross-border transactions with cryptocurrency support

## Pricing

Halo pairs a simple monthly plan with a small per-sale platform fee that drops as you grow. You bring your own Stripe account, so you stay in control of your payments and payouts.

<Pricing productId="halo" />

The platform fee is deducted automatically through Stripe at checkout and is separate from Stripe's own processing fees. Higher plans carry a lower fee, so the more you scale the less you pay per order.

## Payments

Halo uses Stripe Connect: connect your own Stripe account from **Settings → Payments** in a few clicks and start accepting cards. A built-in **test mode** lets you place real end-to-end orders with no money and no real processor, so you can validate your full store before going live.

## Tax

Halo calculates and collects sales tax automatically at checkout via Stripe Tax. Manage your own tax registrations (the regions where you are required to collect) from **Settings → Tax**; tax is then computed per order based on where you are registered. You remain responsible for registering with the relevant tax authorities and remitting what you collect.
