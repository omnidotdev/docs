**Omni Herald** is a transactional and marketing email platform. It sends receipts, notifications, auth emails, and broadcasts from dedicated IPs with managed deliverability, suppression, and per-tenant reputation isolation, natively wired into the Omni event and workflow stack.

## Key Features

- **Transactional send API**: A single idempotent endpoint with verified domains, DKIM signing, and suppression checks before send
- **Delivery tracking**: Every message and its lifecycle (delivered, bounced, complained) in one searchable log
- **Deliverability recommendations**: Per-domain guidance on verification gaps, bounce and complaint rates, and warm-up
- **Suppression management**: Hard bounces and complaints suppress automatically to protect your reputation
- **Marketing broadcasts**: One-click unsubscribe and CAN-SPAM compliant footers built in
- **Events everywhere**: Delivery events stream out as CloudEvents for the rest of your stack to react to
