---
title: Sending Domains
description: Add and verify a sending domain so Herald can sign and send mail as your brand.
---

Before Herald can send on your behalf you must verify a **sending domain**. Verification proves you control the domain and lets Herald sign your mail with DKIM.

## Add a domain

In the dashboard, go to **Domains** and add a domain (for example `mail.yourbrand.com`), or via the SDK:

```ts
await herald.domains.create({ domain: "mail.yourbrand.com" });
```

Herald generates a dedicated DKIM key for the domain and returns the DNS records to publish.

## Publish the DNS records

Add these records at your DNS host:

- **DKIM** — a `TXT` record at `herald._domainkey.<domain>` with the generated public key. Herald signs every message from the domain with the matching private key.
- **SPF** — a `TXT` record at the domain: `v=spf1 include:_spf.herald.omni.dev ~all`. The `_spf.herald.omni.dev` indirection means a change to Herald's sending IPs never requires you to edit your DNS again.
- **DMARC** — a `TXT` record at `_dmarc.<domain>`: `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@omni.dev` to start in monitor mode.

## Verify

Click **Verify** in the dashboard once the records have propagated. Herald checks the DKIM record live and flips the domain to **verified**. Sending from an unverified domain is rejected.

## Ramping DMARC

Start at `p=none` (monitor). Once your DMARC reports are clean, ramp to `p=quarantine` and eventually `p=reject` to protect your domain from spoofing. Herald's deliverability recommendations surface when a domain is ready to ramp.
