---
title: Sequences
description: Automatically drip a timed series of emails to contacts after they join an audience.
---

A sequence is an automated, multi-step email drip. When a contact joins an attached audience, Herald enrolls them and sends each step on a schedule you set, without any manual work. It is the automated counterpart to a one-off [broadcast](/products/herald/broadcasts).

A typical use: an ad drives sign-ups to a lead-capture form, the email lands in an audience, and a 3-8 email sequence welcomes the lead and nurtures them toward a conversion.

## Build a sequence

In the dashboard, go to **Sequences** and create one. Add ordered **steps**; each step is:

- a [template](/products/herald/templates) for the content,
- a **delay** relative to enrollment (a number and a unit — minutes, hours, or days; step 1 is usually "on signup"),
- an optional subject override.

Use **Preview** on a step to see the template it will send.

## Activate and attach

Set a from-address and **Activate** the sequence (this requires your sender name and postal address in Settings, like a broadcast). Then **Attach** it to an audience.

While the sequence is active, every new contact added to that audience is automatically enrolled at step 1, and the drip advances on schedule.

## Enrollment lifecycle

- Each enrolled contact moves step by step until the sequence completes.
- If a contact **unsubscribes** or is **suppressed**, their enrollment exits immediately and no further steps are sent.
- If a contact **converts** (see below), their enrollment exits with reason `converted`, so a lead who has already booked or bought stops receiving the nurture.
- Pausing a sequence holds existing enrollments; reactivating resumes them.

## Stop nurturing on conversion

The point of a nurture is a conversion (a booked call, a purchase), and those usually happen outside Herald, on a scheduler or a checkout page. When that happens you want the lead to stop receiving the rest of the drip.

Give a sequence an optional **goal event**, a short label that names its conversion (for example `booked_call` or `purchased_plan`), set on the sequence when you create or update it through the [API or SDK](/products/herald/api-reference). Then, when the lead converts, have the system that knows about it (your booking page, checkout, or a webhook from your scheduler) call the `recordConversion` mutation with the contact's email address:

```graphql
mutation {
  recordConversion(input: { address: "lead@example.com", event: "booked_call" }) {
    exited
  }
}
```

- With an `event`, only enrollments in sequences whose goal event matches are exited, so a lead who booked a call can still finish a separate purchase nurture.
- Omit `event` for a blanket conversion that exits **every** active enrollment for that address ("they converted, stop everything").
- An unknown address or no match is a harmless no-op that returns `exited: 0`, so a webhook that fires for a non-lead does nothing.

`recordConversion` accepts a full or sending [API key](/products/herald/api-reference), and Herald emits a `herald.contact.converted` event when an enrollment exits, so the rest of your stack can react.

## Getting contacts in

Point your capture form (or CSV import, or the contacts API) at the attached audience. Anything that adds a contact to that audience starts the drip.
