---
title: Sequences
description: Automatically drip a timed series of emails to contacts after they join an audience.
---

A sequence is an automated, multi-step email drip. When a contact joins an attached audience, Herald enrolls them and sends each step on a schedule you set, without any manual work. It is the automated counterpart to a one-off [broadcast](/products/herald/broadcasts).

A typical use: an ad drives sign-ups to a lead-capture form, the email lands in an audience, and a 3-8 email sequence welcomes the lead and nurtures them toward a conversion.

## Build a sequence

In the dashboard, go to **Sequences** and create one. Add ordered **steps**; each step is:

- a [template](/products/herald/templates) for the content,
- a **delay** relative to enrollment (a number and a unit — minutes, hours, or days; step 1 is usually "on signup"),
- an optional subject override.

Use **Preview** on a step to see the template it will send.

## Activate and attach

Set a from-address and **Activate** the sequence (this requires your sender name and postal address in Settings, like a broadcast). Then **Attach** it to an audience.

While the sequence is active, every new contact added to that audience is automatically enrolled at step 1, and the drip advances on schedule.

## Enrollment lifecycle

- Each enrolled contact moves step by step until the sequence completes.
- If a contact **unsubscribes** or is **suppressed**, their enrollment exits immediately and no further steps are sent.
- Pausing a sequence holds existing enrollments; reactivating resumes them.

## Getting contacts in

Point your capture form (or CSV import, or the contacts API) at the attached audience. Anything that adds a contact to that audience starts the drip.
