---
title: Webhooks
description: Receive delivery, bounce, open, and click events at your own endpoint.
---

Webhooks push Herald's message events to your application in real time, so you can react to what happens after a send.

## Register an endpoint

In the dashboard, go to **Webhooks** and register an endpoint URL, or via the SDK:

```ts
await herald.webhooks.create({
  url: "https://yourapp.com/herald",
  eventTypes: ["delivered", "bounced", "opened", "clicked"],
});
```

A **signing secret** is returned once at creation. Store it to verify incoming posts.

## Event types

- `delivered` — the receiving server accepted the message.
- `bounced` — the message hard-bounced.
- `opened` — the recipient opened the message (requires open tracking).
- `clicked` — the recipient clicked a tracked link (requires click tracking).

## Verifying and replaying

Each delivery is signed with your endpoint's secret; verify the signature before trusting a payload. Deliveries are retried with backoff, and you can **replay** a past delivery from the webhook log in the dashboard if your endpoint was down.
