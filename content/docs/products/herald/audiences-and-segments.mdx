title: Audiences and Segments
description: Build contact lists and filter them into reusable segments for targeted sends.
---

An **audience** is a list of contacts. A **segment** is a saved filter over an audience's contacts. Together they let you send to the right subset of people.

## Audiences

In the dashboard, go to **Audiences** and create a list (for example "Newsletter"). Add contacts individually or import a CSV in bulk. Each contact has an email address and an optional bag of **attributes** (merge data such as `first_name`, `plan`, `country`).

Imports are idempotent: re-importing a CSV only adds new addresses, never duplicates, and syntactically invalid addresses are dropped so a bad list can't erode your sending reputation.

You can also add contacts programmatically as your own signup forms capture them (for example a lead-capture form behind an ad), or let visitors subscribe from a form on your website with a [sign-up key](/products/herald/signup-forms).

A contact who signed up through a form with email confirmation turned on shows as **Awaiting confirmation** until they confirm, and receives nothing until then.

## Team audience

A team audience is an audience whose contacts are exactly your workspace's members. Use it to email your own team, such as an internal update or an announcement for staff.

### Turn it on

Only workspace owners and admins can turn on a team audience. Until one exists, owners and admins see a **Create team audience** card on the **Audiences** page, marked **Admin**. Select it and Herald creates an audience named **Team** and fills it with your workspace's members.

Once it is on, the audience shows a **Team** badge and the note **Synced from your workspace members**.

### How it stays in sync

Herald keeps the audience matched to your workspace's members automatically:

- when you turn it on
- when someone joins or leaves the workspace
- every hour

New members are added as subscribed contacts, and members who leave the workspace are removed. A member who unsubscribes stays unsubscribed: the sync never resubscribes anyone. If Herald can't read the member list, the audience keeps its current contacts until the next sync.

Because the members decide who is in it, a team audience has no edit controls. You can't add, remove, or import contacts, or attach a sign-up form.

### Turn off the team audience

To stop using it, open the team audience and select **Turn off team audience** (owners and admins, marked **Admin**), then confirm. Herald removes the audience and its contacts; your workspace members are not affected. It can't be turned off while a broadcast to it is awaiting approval, scheduled, or sending, so cancel or stop that broadcast first. You can turn it on again later, which syncs everyone back.

### Send to your team

Send to your team with a normal [broadcast](/products/herald/broadcasts): pick the **Team** audience in the broadcast's audience picker. It goes through the same request and approval steps as any broadcast, and carries the usual unsubscribe footer.

## Segments

A segment filters an audience by contact attributes. In **Segments**, choose an audience, set the match mode (**all** or **any** conditions), and add conditions:

- `attribute` + operator (`equals`, `does not equal`, `contains`, `greater than`, `less than`, `exists`) + value.

Each saved segment shows a live count of matching contacts. Target a segment in a **broadcast** to reach just that subset instead of the whole audience.

## Attributes power personalization

The same attributes you filter on are the `{{variables}}` used to personalize templates at send time, so "engaged US customers" can each receive their own `{{first_name}}`.
