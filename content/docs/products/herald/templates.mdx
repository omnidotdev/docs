title: Templates
description: Reusable subject and body content with per-recipient variables.
---

A template is a reusable subject and HTML body you can load into the composer, a broadcast, or a sequence step. Templates keep your content in one place instead of pasting it into every send.

## Create a template

In the dashboard, go to **Templates** and create one with a subject and HTML body. Use `{{variable}}` placeholders anywhere in the subject or body:

```html
Subject: Welcome, {{first_name}} 👋
Body:    <p>Thanks for joining, {{first_name}}.</p>
```

The editor detects the variables you reference and lets you preview the rendered result with sample values.

## Edit a template

Click any template in the list to open it in edit mode, change the subject or body, and save. The live preview shows exactly what recipients will see.

## Variables at send time

When a template is used in a broadcast or sequence, each recipient's variables come from their **contact attributes** (see [Audiences and Segments](/docs/products/herald/audiences-and-segments)). A referenced variable with no value renders as an empty string, so a partial data set never breaks a send.
