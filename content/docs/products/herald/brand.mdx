---
title: Brand
description: Set the logo, colors, font, and footer note that wrap every broadcast written in the editor.
---

A brand kit makes every broadcast look like it comes from you, without anyone writing HTML. Herald wraps each broadcast written in the editor in an email-safe branded layout: your logo at the top, the body in your font and colors, your footer note, and the compliance footer.

Only workspace owners and admins can change the brand. In the dashboard, open **Brand** (marked **Admin**). Until a brand is set up, owners and admins see a prompt to set one up on the **Broadcasts** page.

## Logo

Upload a JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF image up to 10 MB. Add alt text, usually your brand name, which screen readers read aloud and email apps show when images are off; the brand can't be saved without it. Set the logo's width between 40 and 300 pixels.

## Colors

Pick an **accent** color (links and buttons), a **background** color, and a **text** color, with the color picker or as a hex value like `#1a2b3c`.

Each color pair must reach a WCAG AA contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 so the email stays readable:

- text on the background
- the accent on white
- the accent on the background

Live badges show each pair's ratio and whether it passes as you edit. A brand with a failing pair can't be saved.

In email apps that honor dark mode, the layout switches to a dark background and lightens links so they stay readable.

## Font

Choose from fonts every email app has (such as System, Helvetica, Verdana, Georgia, and Courier), so the email looks the same everywhere and never falls back to an unexpected font.

## Footer note

Add an optional line of up to 500 characters above the compliance footer, such as your tagline or why people receive your emails.

## Turn on the branded layout

Select **Use branded layout** and save. Broadcasts written in the editor then use the layout; broadcasts written in [custom HTML](/products/herald/broadcasts#custom-html) are sent exactly as written.

The preview beside the form renders a sample email with your unsaved changes, exactly as recipients would get it. It shows the layout even while it is turned off, so the brand can be set up before broadcasts use it.

## Changes and approved broadcasts

A broadcast takes the brand kit as it is when an admin approves it. Brand changes apply to drafts and to broadcasts awaiting approval, but never to a broadcast that was already approved. If the brand changes while an admin reviews a request, the review asks for the latest version before approving.
