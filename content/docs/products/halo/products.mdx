title: Products
description: Build your catalog with options, variants, prices, inventory, collections, and subscriptions.
---

Manage your catalog from **Products** in the dashboard. Products surface on your storefront for customers to browse and buy. A product can be physical or digital (see [Digital Products](/products/halo/digital-products)).

## Products and variants

Each product has a title, slug, description, status (draft or published), tags, and a vendor. Add **options** (for example Size and Color) and Halo tracks a **variant** for each combination, with its own SKU and barcode.

## Prices

Set a price per variant in your store's currency, with an optional compare-at price to show a markdown. A variant can also carry a **recurring** price (billed every day, week, month, or year) to sell it as a subscription product.

## Inventory

Track stock per variant. Each inventory item records whether the variant requires shipping, its weight, cost, origin country, and HS code (used for shipping and customs), and holds an available, reserved, and incoming quantity per location. Stock is reserved when an order is confirmed and released if the order is cancelled.

## Collections

Group products into **collections** (shown as Categories in the dashboard) to organize your storefront. A collection can be **manual**, where you choose the products, or **automatic**, where products are matched by rules. Feature a collection to highlight it on your storefront.

## Media

Upload product images and other media to the media library. On paid plans you can also use video media with poster frames and transcoded MP4. See [Pricing](/products/halo/pricing).

## Subscriptions

When a variant has a recurring price, checkout creates a subscription on your connected Stripe account. Stripe bills each renewal and Halo creates a new order every cycle, so a physical subscription ships again and a digital one re-grants access. The price, cadence, and shipping address are captured at purchase so renewals continue even if the catalog changes.
