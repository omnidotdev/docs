title: Content

Beyond products, Halo includes a content model so your store carries your content as well as your catalog. Define the structure that fits your business, then publish entries, pages, and articles against it.

## Content types and fields

Define your own **content types** under **Settings → Content model**. Each type has a set of **fields**, and every field has a type, configuration, and options such as required and localized. Group fields to organize the editor.

## Entries

Create **entries** of a content type from **Content**. An entry has a status (draft or published), an optional slug and title, a locale, and SEO metadata (title, description, and social image). This is how you publish pages and articles alongside your store.

## Versions and scheduling

Content types keep **version history** by default, so you can track changes to an entry over time. Set a scheduled publish time on an entry to release it automatically in the future.

## Singletons

Mark a content type as a **singleton** when there is only ever one of it, such as a homepage or an about page. A singleton holds a single entry instead of a collection, which is the right shape for one-off pages.

## Media

Upload and manage images and other files in the **media library**, and reference them from your content and products.

## Comments

Readers can leave **comments** on your entries. Comments arrive as pending and you approve them from **Comments** in the dashboard, so nothing appears on your storefront until you have reviewed it.
