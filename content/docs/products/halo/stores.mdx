Halo is multi-tenant: each merchant signs up and gets a store. Every store has its own storefront, catalog, content, and settings, backed by its own currency, timezone, and default locale.

## Branding

Theme your storefront from **Settings → Store**. Set your primary, secondary, background, foreground, and accent colors, choose a light or dark surface, and add a logo. The storefront reads these settings directly so your store carries your brand.

## Policies

Publish your refund, privacy, terms, and shipping policies from **Settings → Store**. A refund and privacy policy are required by the card networks, so set these before you go live.

## Social links

Your storefront footer can display links to your social profiles. The platform and footer icon are inferred from each URL, so you only provide the link itself.

## Custom domain

A store is served from its own slug by default, and you can point your own domain at it so the storefront runs on your brand instead of a shared host. Custom domains are available on every plan.

## Team members

Invite people to help run a store and assign each a role. The free plan includes a single member; paid plans include unlimited members (Halo bills by stores and usage, not per seat). See [Pricing](/products/halo/pricing).
