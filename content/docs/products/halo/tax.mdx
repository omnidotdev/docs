Halo calculates and collects sales tax automatically at checkout via Stripe Tax.

## Registrations

Manage your own tax registrations (the regions where you are required to collect) from **Settings → Tax**. Tax is then computed per order based on where you are registered.

<Callout type="warn">
  You remain responsible for registering with the relevant tax authorities and
  remitting what you collect.
</Callout>
