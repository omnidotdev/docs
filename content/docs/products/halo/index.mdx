**Omni Halo** is a commerce and content platform for creators. Sign up and get a store, sell physical and digital products, model your own content, and publish pages and articles, all while connecting your own Stripe account so you stay in control of your payments and payouts.

## Key Features

- **Storefront and merchant dashboard**: A customer-facing store plus a dashboard to manage your catalog, content, orders, and settings
- **Physical and digital products**: Sell physical goods and digital products (file downloads, license keys, and external links) from the same catalog
- **Content model**: Define your own content types and fields, then publish entries, pages, and articles alongside your store
- **Bring your own Stripe**: Connect your own Stripe account with Stripe Connect and accept cards in a few steps
- **Shipping and tax**: Set shipping zones and rates, and collect sales tax automatically with Stripe Tax
- **Orders and fulfillment**: Track orders through their lifecycle, fulfill with tracking, and handle returns and refunds
- **Test mode**: Place real end-to-end orders with no money before you go live
- **AI agents**: Run store operations through an agent action API and an MCP server (Team tier)
