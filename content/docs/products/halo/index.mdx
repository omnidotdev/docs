**Omni Halo** is a modular ecommerce and CMS platform for creators. Sign up and get a store, sell physical and digital products, model your own content types, and publish pages and posts, all while bringing your own Stripe account so you stay in control of your payments and payouts.

## Key Features

- **Storefront and merchant dashboard**: A customer-facing store plus a dashboard to manage your catalog, content, and settings
- **Physical and digital products**: Sell both physical goods and digital products from the same catalog
- **Content management**: Model your own content types and publish pages and posts alongside your store
- **Bring your own Stripe**: Connect your own Stripe account and start accepting cards in a few clicks
- **Modular architecture**: Build your store with the components you need
- **Self-hostable core**: An open-source core you can run yourself, or use the hosted multi-tenant platform
- **Multi-vendor marketplaces**: Stores can host multiple vendors and sellers
- **Plugin system**: Extend your store with plugins
