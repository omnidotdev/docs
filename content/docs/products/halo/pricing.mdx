Halo pairs a simple monthly plan with a small per-sale platform fee that drops as you grow. You bring your own Stripe account, so you stay in control of your payments and payouts.

<Pricing productId="halo" features />

The platform fee is deducted automatically through Stripe at checkout and is separate from Stripe's own processing fees. Higher plans carry a lower fee, so the more you scale the less you pay per order.
