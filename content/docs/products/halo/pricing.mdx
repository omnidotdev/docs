Halo pairs a simple monthly plan with a small per-sale platform fee that drops as you grow. You connect your own Stripe account, so you stay in control of your payments and payouts.

Plans are billed monthly or yearly, with yearly billing discounted by roughly 20%. Upgrade for more stores, more products, higher media storage and bandwidth, video media, and advanced features. See the relevant pages for what each plan unlocks: [Discounts](/products/halo/discounts) (advanced discounts on Pro and above) and [Agents](/products/halo/agent) (AI agents on Team).

## The platform fee

The **platform fee** is a percentage taken from each storefront sale. It is collected automatically through Stripe as the Connect application fee at checkout, and it drops as you move up plans, so the more you scale the less you pay per order.

The platform fee is separate from Stripe's own card-processing fees. Stripe charges its standard processing fee for handling the payment, and that cost settles on your connected account alongside the platform fee. Because you bring your own Stripe, the money reaches your account directly, net of both fees.
