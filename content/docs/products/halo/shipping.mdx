---
title: Shipping
description: Define shipping zones and flat, price-based, or weight-based rates, matched to the buyer at checkout.
---

Set up shipping from **Settings → Shipping**. Shipping applies to physical products; digital products never require it.

## Shipping origin

Set the address your orders ship from. The origin feeds tax calculation and is where your rates are measured from.

## Zones

A **shipping zone** is a named group of countries. Create a zone for each region you ship to, and attach rates to it. At checkout, Halo matches the buyer's country to a zone and offers that zone's rates.

## Rates

Each zone can carry one or more **rates**. A rate has a name, an estimated delivery window, and one of three matching types:

- **Flat**: A fixed fee for the zone.
- **Price-based**: Applies when the cart total falls inside a value window (for example free shipping over a threshold).
- **Weight-based**: Applies when the cart weight falls inside a weight window, using the per-variant weights from your inventory.

Only active rates in the matching zone are offered, and only those whose value or weight window the cart satisfies.
