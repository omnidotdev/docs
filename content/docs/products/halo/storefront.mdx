Halo pairs a customer-facing storefront with a merchant dashboard. Customers browse and buy on the storefront, while you manage your catalog, content, and settings from the dashboard.

## Stores

Halo is multi-tenant: each merchant signs up and gets a store. Every store has its own storefront and its own dashboard. If you would rather run the stack yourself, an open-source core is available for self-hosting.

Stores can also host multiple vendors and sellers, so you can run a multi-vendor marketplace rather than a single-seller shop.

## Products

Sell physical and digital products from the same catalog. Manage your products from the merchant dashboard, where they surface on your storefront for customers to purchase.

## Content types

Beyond products, Halo lets you model your own content types. Define the structure that fits your business and manage that content alongside your catalog.

## Publishing

Publish pages and posts to build out your storefront around your products, so your store carries your content as well as your catalog.

## Extending Halo

Halo is modular. Build your store with the components you need, and extend it further with plugins.
