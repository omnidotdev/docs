Halo uses Stripe Connect: connect your own Stripe account from **Settings → Payments** in a few clicks and start accepting cards. Because you bring your own Stripe, you stay in control of your payments and payouts.

## Test mode

A built-in **test mode** lets you place real end-to-end orders with no money and no real processor, so you can validate your full store before going live.

<Callout type="info">
  Switch out of test mode when you are ready to accept real orders.
</Callout>
