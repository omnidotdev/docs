<Callout type="warn">
  Halo is in early development. These steps describe the intended flow and will
  change as the product matures.
</Callout>

Halo is a hosted, multi-tenant platform: you sign up and get your own store. An open-source core is also available if you would rather self-host.

## 1. Create your store

Sign up and get a store of your own. Your store comes with a customer-facing storefront and a merchant dashboard where you manage your catalog, content, and settings.

## 2. Connect Stripe

Halo brings your own Stripe. From **Settings → Payments**, connect your Stripe account so you can start accepting cards. See [Payments](/products/halo/payments) for details, including built-in test mode.

## 3. Add products

Add physical and digital products to your catalog from the merchant dashboard. See [Storefront](/products/halo/storefront) for how products and content fit together.

## 4. Model content and publish

Model your own content types and publish pages and posts alongside your store. See [Storefront](/products/halo/storefront).

## 5. Configure tax

If you collect sales tax, add your tax registrations from **Settings → Tax** so tax is calculated automatically at checkout. See [Tax](/products/halo/tax).

## 6. Go live

Once your store is set up and Stripe is connected, switch out of test mode and start taking real orders. Review [Pricing](/products/halo/pricing) for plan details and the per-sale platform fee.
