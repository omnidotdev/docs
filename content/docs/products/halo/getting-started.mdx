Halo is a hosted, multi-tenant platform: you sign up and get your own store with a customer-facing storefront and a merchant dashboard. This guide takes you from a new account to a live store taking real orders.

## 1. Create your store

Sign in with your Omni account, then create a store under your workspace. Each store has its own storefront, catalog, content, and settings, and is served from its own slug (and optionally your own domain, see [Stores](/products/halo/stores)).

## 2. Connect Stripe

From **Settings → Payments**, connect your own Stripe account through Stripe Connect. Because you bring your own Stripe, you stay in control of your payments and payouts. See [Payments](/products/halo/payments) for the onboarding flow and built-in test mode.

## 3. Add products

Add physical and digital products from **Products**. Give each product options and variants, set prices, and track inventory. See [Products](/products/halo/products), and [Digital Products](/products/halo/digital-products) for downloads, license keys, and external links.

## 4. Model content and publish

Define your own content types and fields under **Settings → Content model**, then create entries, pages, and articles under **Content**. See [Content](/products/halo/content).

## 5. Configure shipping and tax

Set up shipping zones and rates under **Settings → Shipping**, and add your tax registrations under **Settings → Tax** so sales tax is calculated automatically at checkout. See [Shipping](/products/halo/shipping) and [Tax](/products/halo/tax).

## 6. Go live

Validate the full checkout with [test mode](/products/halo/payments) first. When everything looks right, switch test mode off and start taking real orders. Review [Pricing](/products/halo/pricing) for plan details and the per-sale platform fee.

## Finding your way around

The dashboard sidebar is your home base once a store is active:

| Section | What it is for |
|---------|----------------|
| **Dashboard** | Revenue, order count, average order value, and top products at a glance |
| **Products** | Your catalog: products, options, variants, prices, and inventory |
| **Categories** | Collections that group products on your storefront |
| **Content** | Entries, singleton pages, and the media library |
| **Orders** | Every order, its status, fulfillments, and refunds |
| **Customers** | The people who have bought from your store |
| **Comments** | Reader comments on your content, with moderation |
| **Marketing** | Discount codes and email subscribers |
| **Settings** | Store branding, content model, navigation, payments, shipping, tax, and billing |
