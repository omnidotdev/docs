---
title: Verification
description: Get verified as an open-source project or nonprofit for a 0% platform fee
---

Verified open-source projects and nonprofits pay a **0% platform fee** on
Crystal, instead of the flat 4% that applies to every other account. The 0% rate
is unlocked by verifying your workspace; it is reviewed by the Crystal team, not
granted automatically.

<Callout type="info">
  Verification only affects the **platform fee**. Stripe's standard payment
  processing fee still applies to each payment, since that covers the card
  processing itself. See [payments and fees](/products/crystal/payments-fees).
</Callout>

## Who qualifies

Two kinds of workspace can be verified:

- **Open-source project** a project released under an open-source license
- **Nonprofit** a registered nonprofit organization

## Request verification

An owner or admin of the workspace requests verification from **Settings** for
that workspace, in the section inviting FOSS and nonprofit organizations to
apply for a 0% fee. Expand it and submit the request:

1. **Choose your workspace type**, open-source project or nonprofit.
2. **Provide evidence** for that type:
   - For an **open-source project**, a link that shows the project is open
     source, such as the repository, organization, project site, or package page.
   - For a **nonprofit**, your registered legal name and registration number
     (for example an EIN).
3. **Add anything else** that helps the review (optional), such as the license,
   jurisdiction, or supporting links.
4. **Attest that the information is truthful.** Misrepresenting eligibility for
   the reduced fee can result in verification being revoked.
5. Submit for review.

## After you submit

Your request goes into review and shows as pending. The team decides each
request by hand, and you are emailed when it is decided.

- **Approved** your workspace is verified and the 0% platform fee takes effect.
  The status shows the date your verification is valid until.
- **Not approved** the status shows the reason, and an owner or admin can
  correct the request and submit again.

## Keeping verification current

Verification is time-bound rather than permanent. As the expiry date approaches,
the Settings section prompts an owner or admin to **re-attest** that the
workspace still qualifies, which renews the 0% fee for another period. If
verification lapses, the standard 4% platform fee applies until it is renewed.
