Payments on Crystal flow directly to your own Stripe account, never through a
shared pool. That means you set this up **first**: until a Stripe account is
connected and verified, your public page shows "Coming soon" and no one can
support you.

## Connect Stripe

1. Open **Payouts** from the sidebar.
2. In the **Stripe Connect** card, choose **Connect with Stripe**.
3. You are taken to Stripe to create your account. Provide the business and
   bank details Stripe asks for. This is where your money will land.
4. When you finish, Stripe returns you to Crystal.

Your account starts as **Pending Verification**. If you leave before finishing,
use **Continue Setup** on the Payouts page to pick up where you left off, and
**Refresh status** to re-check once Stripe has processed your details.

## Verify your identity

Stripe verifies every account before it can take payments or send payouts, in
line with financial regulations. Verification usually takes only a few minutes,
though some details can take longer to clear on Stripe's side. Once you are
**Verified**, your balance appears and your public page opens for support.

You can open your **Stripe Dashboard** from the Payouts page at any time to
manage bank details, tax information, and account settings directly with Stripe.

## Balances and withdrawing

The Payouts page shows three figures:

| Balance | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Available** | Cleared funds ready to withdraw |
| **Pending** | Recent payments still settling |
| **Total Earned** | Your lifetime earnings across everything |

Choose **Request Payout** to withdraw from your available balance to your bank.
Payouts typically arrive in 2 to 7 business days depending on your bank and
country. You can review every past withdrawal, its amount, and its status under
**Payout history** (also mirrored on the **Earnings** page).

## Fees

Crystal takes a flat 4% platform fee per transaction (0% for verified
open-source projects and nonprofits), plus standard Stripe payment processing,
which you bear. Both come out before funds reach your available balance. See
[payments and fees](/products/crystal/payments-fees) for the full detail.

## Taxes: you are the merchant of record

This part matters, so we are direct about it.

<Callout type="warn">
  On Crystal, **you are the merchant of record** for the money your supporters
  send. Crystal facilitates the payment and routes it to your Stripe account; it
  does not sell on your behalf. You are responsible for your own taxes.
</Callout>

- **Crystal does not calculate, collect, or remit taxes for you.** Any VAT, GST,
  or sales tax owed on the support you receive is yours to register for, charge,
  and remit in the jurisdictions where you have obligations.
- **Keep your own records.** Use your earnings data and your Stripe Dashboard to
  gather the figures you need at filing time.
- **US creators:** Stripe issues a **1099-K** to qualifying US Connect accounts
  that pass the reporting threshold, and files it with the IRS. Find it in your
  Stripe Dashboard. This is Stripe's standard tax reporting, separate from any
  income tax you owe.

If you are unsure about your obligations, talk to a qualified tax professional.
Crystal cannot give tax advice.
