Crystal is free to use. There are no plans to buy and no monthly bill: the only
thing you pay is a small fee on each payment your supporters make. This page
covers that fee, plus how currency works.

## Your plan

Crystal has a single free plan with every feature included: unlimited membership
tiers, posts, and funding goals, plus the full analytics dashboard. Nothing is
locked behind a subscription, so there is no plan to choose or upgrade.

Crystal is funded by the flat fee on supporter payments below, so the platform
earns only when you do.

## Fees on each payment

A flat **<PlatformFee productId="crystal" /> platform fee** is taken from each payment
a supporter makes (memberships, donations, gifts, and digital sales). The same
rate applies to every account. Verified open-source projects and nonprofits
pay **0%**:

| Workspace type | Platform fee |
|-------------------|--------------|
| Verified open-source project | 0% |
| Verified nonprofit | 0% |
| All other accounts | <PlatformFee productId="crystal" /> |

The 0% rate is verification-gated: it applies once your workspace is verified
as an open-source project or a nonprofit.

On top of the platform fee, **Stripe charges its own standard payment processing
fee** for handling the card payment, and that processing cost is yours to bear.
Both the platform fee and the Stripe processing fee are deducted before the money reaches
your available balance, so what you withdraw is already net of fees. You can see
the net figures in your Stripe Dashboard.

## Currency and availability

Crystal supports creators in the **United States and Canada**: receiving support
needs a Stripe account and bank details in one of those countries. Amounts are
handled in US dollars, and Stripe settles Canadian accounts in Canadian dollars
on payout. Donations start at a $1 minimum. Supporters can pay from anywhere.
Payouts settle to your connected bank account in line with your Stripe account's
configuration. More countries are planned.

## Taxes

Fees are not taxes. You are the merchant of record for the money your supporters
send, which means any VAT, GST, or sales tax is your responsibility to handle.
Crystal does not calculate, collect, or remit taxes for you. See the tax section
of [payouts](/products/crystal/payouts) for the full detail, including Stripe's
1099-K reporting for qualifying US accounts.
