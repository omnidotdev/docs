Crystal has two separate money conversations: the **plan** you pay for Crystal
itself, and the **fees** taken from each payment your supporters make. This page
covers both, plus how currency works.

## Your Crystal plan

Your plan sets how much you can build: how many tiers and goals you can publish,
whether you get analytics, custom branding, and API access. Prices are billed to
your organization.

<Pricing productId="crystal" features fee={false} />

Manage or upgrade your plan under **Settings** for your organization, where
**Manage Billing** opens the billing portal to update your payment method and
view invoices.

## Fees on each payment

Separately from your plan, a flat **<PlatformFee productId="crystal" /> platform fee** is taken from each payment
a supporter makes (memberships, donations, gifts, and digital sales). The same
rate applies to every paid account. Verified open-source projects and nonprofits
pay **0%**:

| Organization type | Platform fee |
|-------------------|--------------|
| Verified open-source project | 0% |
| Verified nonprofit | 0% |
| All other accounts | <PlatformFee productId="crystal" /> |

The 0% rate is verification-gated: it applies once your organization is verified
as an open-source project or a nonprofit.

On top of the platform fee, **Stripe charges its own standard payment processing
fee** for handling the card payment, and that processing cost is yours to bear.
Both the platform fee and the Stripe processing fee are deducted before the money reaches
your available balance, so what you withdraw is already net of fees. You can see
the net figures in your Stripe Dashboard.

## Currency

Amounts on Crystal are handled in US dollars. Donations start at a $1 minimum.
Payouts settle to your connected bank account in line with your Stripe account's
configuration.

## Taxes

Fees are not taxes. You are the merchant of record for the money your supporters
send, which means any VAT, GST, or sales tax is your responsibility to handle.
Crystal does not calculate, collect, or remit taxes for you. See the tax section
of [payouts](/products/crystal/payouts) for the full detail, including Stripe's
1099-K reporting for qualifying US accounts.
