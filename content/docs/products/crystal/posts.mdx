title: Posts

---

Posts are how you keep your audience in the loop: project updates, behind-the-
scenes notes, release announcements, or content reserved for the people backing
you. Each post's visibility is yours to set, so the same feed can carry public
news and members-only material.

## Write a post

Open **Posts** from the sidebar and choose **Create New Post**. A post has:

- **Title** and **content**, the body of the update
- **Excerpt** (optional), a short summary shown in previews
- **Visibility**, who can read the full post (see below)

New posts are created as **drafts**. A draft is private to you until you
**publish** it, so you can write ahead and release when you are ready. You can
unpublish a post later to pull it back to a draft, edit it at any time, or delete
it (deleting is confirmed first and cannot be undone).

## Visibility

Each post sets how much of it the public sees:

| Visibility | Who sees the full post |
|------------|------------------------|
| **Public** | Anyone |
| **Supporters only** | Any active supporter of your workspace |
| **Tier-gated** | Supporters on a chosen minimum [membership tier](/products/crystal/membership-tiers) or higher |

For a tier-gated post, pick the minimum tier that unlocks it. Everyone else sees
the post as a locked teaser, an incentive to back you or upgrade, while the full
content stays reserved for the supporters who qualify.

## Where supporters read them

Your published posts appear in the **Updates** feed on your public page, at
`/@yourhandle/posts`. Anyone can browse the feed: public posts render in full,
and gated posts show as locked teasers. When a supporter is signed in, their
session automatically unlocks the posts their tier clears, resolved server-side,
so they never see content they have not earned.

## Plan limits

How many posts you can publish each month depends on your Crystal plan: 10 per
month on Free, and unlimited on Pro and Team. See
[payments and fees](/products/crystal/payments-fees) for the full comparison.
