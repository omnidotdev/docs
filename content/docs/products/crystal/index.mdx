**Omni Crystal** is a low-fee funding platform for creators, with a flat 4% platform fee (0% for verified open-source projects and nonprofits) plus standard Stripe payment processing. It enables tip jars, paid links, membership gates, and repository bounties with direct payouts via Stripe Connect.
