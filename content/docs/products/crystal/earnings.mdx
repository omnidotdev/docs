The **Earnings** page is where you see how your creator business is doing:
revenue at a glance, your recurring support, and every payout to your bank
account in one place. Open it from the sidebar once a workspace is active.

## Revenue overview

The top of the page summarizes your workspace's money:

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Revenue over the last 30 days |
| **Recurring** | Your current monthly recurring support |
| **Donations** | One-time [donations](/products/crystal/donations) over the last 30 days |
| **Available** | The balance ready to withdraw now |

Amounts are shown in US dollars. The **Available** figure matches your
withdrawable balance under [payouts](/products/crystal/payouts), already net of
the platform and Stripe processing fees.

## Payout history

Below the overview, the **Payout history** lists every withdrawal to your bank:
its date, amount, and status (completed, processing, pending, or failed). This is
the same history shown on the [payouts](/products/crystal/payouts) page, so you
can review past withdrawals wherever you happen to be.

## Advanced analytics

The overview above gives you the headline numbers. A fuller **analytics
dashboard** is included on the **Pro** and **Team** plans for a deeper look at
your revenue and growth. See [payments and fees](/products/crystal/payments-fees)
for the plan comparison.
