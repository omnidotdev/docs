---
title: Funding Goals
description: Set funding milestones and show supporters your progress
---

Funding goals turn a fundraising target into something supporters can rally
behind. Each goal has a title, a target amount, and a running total that shows
how close you are.

## Create a goal

1. Open the **Manage Funding Goals** page.
2. Choose **Create New Goal**.
3. Enter the details:
   - **Title** what the goal is for, for example "New microphone" or "Go
     full-time"
   - **Description** a short explanation of what reaching it unlocks
   - **Target amount** and **currency**
4. Save. The goal begins tracking progress from zero.

## Manage your goals

From the same page you can:

- **Edit** a goal's title, description, or target
- **Reorder** goals with the up and down arrows
- **Delete** a goal you no longer need

A goal is marked complete once its running total reaches the target. Active
goals appear alongside your tiers on your public support surface, so supporters
can see the progress bar and what their contribution moves toward.

## How many goals you can set

Your Crystal plan sets the limit: 2 on Free, 10 on Pro, and unlimited on Team.
See [payments and fees](/products/crystal/payments-fees) for the full comparison.
