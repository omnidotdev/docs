Digital products let you sell downloads to your audience: files, presets,
templates, ebooks, sample packs, or anything else that ships as a file. Each sale
is a one-time purchase, and the file is delivered securely after payment.

## Add a product

1. Open **Digital Products** from the sidebar and choose **New Product**.
2. Provide the details:
   - **Name** what you are selling
   - **Description** optional, to explain what the buyer gets
   - **Price** in USD
   - **File** the download itself, uploaded from your computer
3. Save. Your product appears on your public page for supporters to buy.

## Manage your products

From the Digital Products page you can **edit** a product's name, description, or
price, and **delete** products you no longer sell.

## How buyers purchase and download

Your active products show up on your public page. A buyer:

1. Chooses a product and enters their email
2. Pays through Stripe Checkout
3. Is offered the download immediately after payment, through a secure,
   time-limited link

<Callout type="info">
  Download links are gated: a link only works for a genuine, completed purchase,
  and it expires. Your source files are never exposed publicly.
</Callout>

## Fees and payouts

Like every payment on Crystal, digital sales route to your Stripe balance after
the platform and processing fees in
[payments and fees](/products/crystal/payments-fees), and are withdrawn through
[payouts](/products/crystal/payouts). As with all your earnings, you are the
merchant of record and responsible for any applicable taxes.
