---
title: Credits
description: How AI credits pay for generation in Keystone, and how to buy more
---

Generation in Keystone is paid for with **AI credits**, one balance per
workspace shared across every Omni product. [AI Credits](/help/ai-credits)
explains how the balance works, when included credits arrive and expire, and
how AI credits differ from account credit. This page covers what is specific to
Keystone.

## Which balance pays

Every turn spends credits from the workspace the site belongs to: your
**personal workspace** for a new site, or the workspace you published the site
into, such as a Team workspace. The builder shows that workspace's balance. See
[Workspaces and Sites](/products/keystone/workspaces).

## What spends credits

Only sending a prompt spends credits. A turn's cost depends on:

- **The model**, more capable models cost more per turn. See [Models](/products/keystone/models)
- **The size of the site and of the change**, every turn reads the whole
  current site but rewrites only the pages it changes, so bigger sites and
  sweeping changes spend more per turn

Picking a starter, selecting a follow-up suggestion, previewing, downloading
your code, publishing, and syncing to GitHub never spend credits.

## Check your balance

The builder header shows your balance (on wider screens), for example
**1,250 AI credits, shared across Omni products**. Select it to open the workspace's
[Billing page](/products/keystone/workspaces#workspace-billing).

## Running out

When the balance reaches zero, generation stops with **You're out of AI
credits. Buy more credits to keep generating.** and a **Buy credits** link.
There is no automatic overage charge on any plan: buy more credits to keep
generating, or, on a paid plan, wait for its included credits to renew.

## Get more credits

- **Included credits**: Pro and Team include a monthly credit allowance; Free
  includes no credits. See [Plans](/products/keystone/plans#limits-by-plan),
  and [Included credits](/help/ai-credits#included-credits) for when it arrives
- **Buy credits**: one-time credit packs, bought in your Omni account. Select
  **Buy credits** in the builder or on the workspace's Billing page; credits
  you buy join the workspace's shared balance

On a workspace's Billing page, only owners and admins can buy credits. See
[Buying credits](/help/ai-credits#buying-credits).
