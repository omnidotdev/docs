---
title: Workspaces and Sites
description: Where your sites live, how many you can keep, and how workspace members and billing work
---

Keystone uses your Omni workspaces, the same ones you use across every Omni
product. A workspace has a plan, an Omni credit balance, and members.

## Your building workspace

New sites start in your **personal workspace**, and its plan sets how many
sites you can keep (see [Sites](#sites)).

When you publish, you can choose another workspace you belong to, such as a Team
workspace. The site then moves into that workspace:

- That workspace's plan decides whether the site gets a hosted site and a
  custom domain. See [Publishing](/products/keystone/publishing#choose-a-workspace)
- Every later turn on the site spends that workspace's
  [Omni credits](/products/keystone/credits), so a Team's members build its
  sites on the Team's credits

If your account has no workspace at all, generation stops with **Generation
needs a workspace**. Create or join one from your Omni account, then try again.

## Sites

Your site, its pages, and the chat transcript are saved as you go. Each plan
has a site limit:

| Plan | Sites |
|------|-------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Team | Unlimited |

Signed in, Keystone opens on **Your sites**, which lists every site you have
built across your workspaces. Select **Open in builder** on a site to keep
working on it, or **New site** to start another.

**New site** starts a new site until you reach the limit. An unused blank site is reused rather than
counted again. At the limit, the builder shows **Picking up where you left off**
and reopens your most recent site, with its conversation and preview, instead
of starting a blank one.

<Callout type="info">
  The builder does not have a list of saved sites yet, so it cannot switch back
  to an earlier site. Publish a site or [download its code](/products/keystone/preview#download-your-code)
  before starting the next one.
</Callout>

## Members

Workspace members and their roles are managed in your Omni account, so they stay
the same across every Omni product. A workspace's **Members** page in Keystone
links to **Manage members in Omni**.

## Workspace billing

Each workspace has a **Billing** page in Keystone. Open it by selecting the
credit balance in the builder header (on wider screens), or from the workspace's
page under **Workspaces** in the command palette (**⌘ K** / **Ctrl K**).

- **Omni credits** shows the workspace's balance. **Buy credits** opens billing
  in your Omni account with the workspace selected.
- **Plan** shows the workspace's current plan. **Upgrade plan** opens the
  [pricing page](https://keystone.omni.dev/pricing), and **Manage subscription**
  opens the billing portal for an existing subscription.

Only workspace owners and admins can buy credits or change the plan. Other
members see the balance and the plan.
