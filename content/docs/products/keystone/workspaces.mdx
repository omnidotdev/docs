title: Workspaces and Sites
description: Where your sites live, how many you can keep, and how workspace members, seats, and billing work
---

Keystone uses your Omni workspaces, the same ones you use across every Omni
product. A workspace has a plan, an AI credit balance, and members.

## Your building workspace

Every site belongs to one workspace. That workspace's plan sets how many sites
it can keep (see [Sites](#sites)), every turn on the site spends that
workspace's [AI credits](/products/keystone/credits), and the credit balance
in the builder header is that workspace's balance.

New sites start in your **personal workspace** unless you pick another one.

### Build in a team workspace

To build a site directly in a team workspace you belong to, either:

- Open the workspace's page and select **New site in this workspace**, then
  use a [template](/products/keystone/templates) or select **Describe one
  from scratch**, or
- In the builder, before the first prompt, pick the workspace under
  **Building in** at the top of the chat panel. The picker shows when you
  belong to more than one workspace

The new site belongs to that workspace from the start, so the team's members
build it on the team's credits and the team's site limit applies.

### Move a site when you publish

When you publish, you can also choose another workspace you belong to. The site
then moves into that workspace, which must have room under its site limit. A
site hosted on Fractal moves its hosting into that workspace's own Fractal
project, where the workspace's Fractal plan applies. See
[Publishing](/products/keystone/publishing#choose-a-workspace).

### Your workspace on Fractal and Arbor

A site you host is your workspace's, on the Omni platform: it runs in the
workspace's own Fractal project (**Keystone sites**) and its code lives in a
repository the workspace owns on Arbor. Every member sees the site in the
[Fractal dashboard](https://fractal.omni.dev) with Fractal's usual roles, and
hosting is billed by the workspace's Fractal plan, only for sites it hosts.
Building stays on the workspace's Keystone plan. See
[Host on Fractal](/products/keystone/publishing#host-on-fractal).

If your account has no workspace at all, generation stops with **Generation
needs a workspace**. Create or join one from your Omni account, then try again.

## Sites

Your site, its pages, and the chat transcript are saved as you go. Each plan
has a site limit:

| Plan | Sites |
|------|-------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Team | Unlimited |

Signed in, Keystone opens on **What will you build?**: a prompt for a new site,
with example sites that fill it in one tap. Below it, **Your sites** lists every
site you have built across your workspaces. Select a site's card to keep
working on it in the builder; a hosted site's card also links to the live site
(**Visit**) and to **Manage in Fractal**. Describe a new site in the prompt, or
select **Browse templates** to open the
[template gallery](/products/keystone/templates). **Your sites** is also in the
header and in the account menu.

A new site can start until the workspace reaches its limit. An unused blank
site is reused rather than counted again. At the limit, **Use this template**
explains the limit instead of creating a site, and the builder shows
**Picking up where you left off** and reopens the workspace's most recent site,
with its conversation and preview, instead of starting a blank one. Each
workspace has its own limit, so a full personal workspace does not stop you
from starting a site in a team workspace.

## Members

Workspace members and their roles are managed in your Omni account, so they stay
the same across every Omni product. A workspace's **Members** page in Keystone
links to **Manage members in Omni**.

## Seats

A workspace's plan includes a number of seats, and every member of a team
workspace takes one:

| Plan | Seats |
|------|-------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 1 |
| Team | 10 |

While a team workspace has no more members than its plan's seats, every member
can build in it. When it has more, starting a site and generating in that
workspace stop with a message such as **This workspace has 12 members, but its
plan includes 10 seats**, with links to the workspace's plan and members. To
keep building there, remove members or upgrade the plan. Existing sites stay
saved, and their published links keep working.

Sharing a workspace with other people therefore needs the Team plan. Your
personal workspace only ever has you in it, so seats never limit it.

## Workspace billing

Each workspace has a **Billing and credits** page in Keystone. Open it by
selecting the credit balance in the builder header (on wider screens), or from
the workspace's page, which you reach from **Workspaces** in the account menu or
the command palette (**⌘ K** / **Ctrl K**).

- **AI credits** shows the workspace's balance. **Buy credits** opens billing
  in your Omni account with the workspace selected.
- **Plan** shows the workspace's current plan. **Upgrade plan** opens the
  [pricing page](https://keystone.omni.dev/pricing), and **Manage subscription**
  opens the billing portal for an existing subscription.

Only workspace owners and admins can buy credits or change the plan. Other
members see the balance and the plan.
