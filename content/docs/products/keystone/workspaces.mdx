---
title: Workspaces and Sites
description: Where your sites live, how many you can keep, and how workspace members, seats, and billing work
---

Keystone uses your Omni workspaces, the same ones you use across every Omni
product. A workspace has a plan, an AI credit balance, and members.

## Your building workspace

Every site belongs to one workspace. That workspace's plan sets how many sites
it can keep (see [Sites](#sites)), every turn on the site spends that
workspace's [AI credits](/products/keystone/credits), and the credit balance
in the builder header is that workspace's balance.

New sites start in your **personal workspace** unless you pick another one. On
each device, the builder remembers the workspace you last picked.

### Build in a team workspace

To build a site directly in a team workspace you belong to, either:

- Open the workspace's page and select **New site in this workspace**, then
  use a [template](/products/keystone/templates) or select **Describe one
  from scratch**, or
- In the builder, before the first prompt, pick the workspace under
  **Building in** at the top of the chat panel. The picker shows when you
  belong to more than one workspace

Picking a workspace is instant: nothing is created until you send your first
prompt, which creates the site in the picked workspace. Beside the picker, the
builder shows how many of the workspace's sites are used (for example, **1 of
1 sites used**), and the header shows the credit balance its turns will spend.
If the workspace has no room for another site, or has more members than its
plan's seats, the builder says so before you type, with links to upgrade its
plan on its billing page, open its latest site, or check its members.

The new site belongs to that workspace from the start, so the team's members
build it on the team's credits and the team's site limit applies. Once the site
exists, **Building in** shows its workspace and links to the workspace's page.

### Move a site to another workspace

A site that is not hosted (a draft, or one shared by preview link) can move to
another workspace you belong to: select **Move to workspace** in its card's
menu under **Your sites**, or pick another workspace when you publish it. The
site moves with its chat, versions, and form submissions. The new workspace
must have room under its site limit and a seat for each of its members; the
site then counts toward its limit, and its turns spend that workspace's
credits. Form notifications go to the member who moved it. A
[GitHub Sync](/products/keystone/github-sync) link made through the old
workspace's GitHub connection is removed, so link the repository again from the
new workspace.

A hosted site stays in the workspace it is hosted in. Its service, address,
custom domain, and code all belong to that workspace on Fractal and Arbor, so
moving it would take the live site offline. To build it elsewhere, start a new
site in the other workspace. In a team workspace, only owners and admins can
move its sites out.

### Your workspace on Fractal and Arbor

A site you host is your workspace's, on the Omni platform: it runs in the
workspace's own Fractal project (**Keystone sites**) and its code lives in a
repository the workspace owns on Arbor. Every member sees the site in the
[Fractal dashboard](https://fractal.omni.dev) with Fractal's usual roles, and
hosting is billed by the workspace's Fractal plan, only for sites it hosts.
Building stays on the workspace's Keystone plan. See
[Host on Fractal](/products/keystone/publishing#host-on-fractal).

If your account has no workspace at all, generation stops with **Generation
needs a workspace**. Create or join one from your Omni account, then try again.

## Sites

Your site, its pages, and the chat transcript are saved as you go. Each plan
has a site limit:

| Plan | Sites |
|------|-------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Team | Unlimited |

Signed in, Keystone opens on **What will you build?**: a prompt for a new site,
with [example prompts](/products/keystone/starters#pick-a-starter) that fill it
in one tap. Below it, **Your sites** lists every
site you have built across your workspaces, each card topped with a live
thumbnail of its home page, its status (**Draft**, **Preview**, or **Live**),
and when it was last edited. Select a site's card to keep working on it in the
builder. A published site's card also links to it with **Visit** (a hosted
site's live address, or its preview link), and a hosted site's card links to
**Manage in Fractal**. Describe a new site in the prompt, or
select **Browse templates** to open the
[template gallery](/products/keystone/templates). **Your sites** is also in the
header and in the account menu.

With two or more sites, a search box above the cards narrows them by name or
live address as you type, ignoring case. Every word you type must match, in any
order. Press Escape or select **Clear search** to show every site again.

Only sites with something built on them count toward the limit. A new site can
start until the workspace reaches its limit. At the limit, Keystone refuses to
start another site there: the builder explains it as soon as the workspace is
picked, with **Upgrade plan** (the workspace's billing page) and a link to open
its most recent site, and **Use this template** explains the limit with a link
to the workspace's plans and billing instead of creating a site. A prompt never
lands on an existing site by accident. Each workspace has its own limit, so a
full personal workspace does not stop you from starting a site in a team
workspace. Deleting a site frees its place.

### Manage your sites

Each card under **Your sites** (and on a workspace's page) has a menu:

- **Rename** gives the site a name of up to 80 characters. The name shows on
  its card and in the builder header in place of the site's generated title;
  the title visitors see in their browser stays as Keystone wrote it
- **Move to workspace** moves a site that is not hosted (see
  [Move a site to another workspace](#move-a-site-to-another-workspace))
- **Delete** deletes the site after you confirm. Deleting removes the site, its
  versions, its form submissions, and its preview link, and takes a hosted site
  offline, releasing its address and any custom domain. It cannot be undone. A
  hosted site's code stays in the workspace's repository on Arbor, where
  workspace owners can delete it, and a GitHub repository linked with
  [GitHub Sync](/products/keystone/github-sync) is left as it is

In a team workspace, only owners and admins can delete or move its sites; every
member can rename them.

## Members

Workspace members and their roles are managed in your Omni account, so they stay
the same across every Omni product. A workspace's **Members** page in Keystone
links to **Manage members in Omni**.

## Seats

A workspace's plan includes a number of seats, and every member of a team
workspace takes one:

| Plan | Seats |
|------|-------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 1 |
| Team | 10 |

While a team workspace has no more members than its plan's seats, every member
can build in it. When it has more, starting a site and generating in that
workspace stop with a message such as **This workspace has 12 members, but its
plan includes 10 seats**, with links to the workspace's plan and members. To
keep building there, remove members or upgrade the plan. Existing sites stay
saved, and their published links keep working.

Sharing a workspace with other people therefore needs the Team plan. Your
personal workspace only ever has you in it, so seats never limit it.

## Workspace billing

Each workspace has a **Billing and credits** page in Keystone. Open it by
selecting the credit balance in the builder header (on wider screens), or from
the workspace's page, which you reach from **Workspaces** in the account menu or
the command palette (**⌘ K** / **Ctrl K**).

- **AI credits** shows the workspace's balance. **Buy credits** opens billing
  in your Omni account with the workspace selected.
- **Plan** shows the workspace's current plan. **Upgrade plan** opens the
  [pricing page](https://keystone.omni.dev/pricing), and **Manage subscription**
  opens the billing portal for an existing subscription.

Only workspace owners and admins can buy credits or change the plan. Other
members see the balance and the plan.
