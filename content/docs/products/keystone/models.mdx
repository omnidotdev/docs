Keystone generates sites with Claude models, served through
[Synapse](/products/synapse), Omni's AI gateway. To choose one, open
**Options** (the sliders button beside send in the builder) and pick a
**Quality**.

## Available models

| Quality | Model | Best for |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Fast** | Haiku 5.5 | Quick drafts and small edits, such as copy and color tweaks |
| **Balanced** (default) | Sonnet 5.5 | Polished sites for most needs |
| **Most capable** | Opus 5.5 | Richer layouts and more detail, for ambitious sites and complex changes |

The list comes from the gateway, so the models on offer can change over time.
The Options menu always shows the current set, and names the model behind each
choice.

## Switch between turns

Each turn uses the quality selected when you send it, and Keystone remembers
your choice on that device. A common pattern is to start a site with **Most
capable**, then switch to **Fast** for small tweaks such as copy edits and
color changes.

## Cost

Models are priced differently, and the Options menu shows how each compares
with the default: a note such as **2x credits** for a model that costs more per turn, or
**fewer credits** for one that costs less. A turn's cost also grows with the
size of your site and of the change, since every turn reads the whole site and
rewrites the pages it changes. See
[Credits](/products/keystone/credits).
