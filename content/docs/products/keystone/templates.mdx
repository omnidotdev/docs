A template is a complete, ready-made website: real pages, real copy, and a
finished design. Starting from one gives you a working site straight away,
which you then make your own by chat. Starting from a template spends no
credits.

## Browse the gallery

Open the gallery from any of these places:

- **Templates** in the site header, or [keystone.omni.dev/templates](https://keystone.omni.dev/templates)
- **New site** on [Your sites](/products/keystone/workspaces#sites), or **New site in this workspace** on a workspace's page
- The builder's preview panel, before your first message
- The template cards on the [Keystone home page](https://keystone.omni.dev)

Each card shows a live, scaled preview of the template's home page. Filter the
gallery by category: **All**, **Business**, **Store**, **Portfolio**, **Blog**,
**Events**, or **Software**.

## Preview a template

Select a card (or **Preview**) to open the full-size preview. Switch between
the template's pages with the page tabs, or follow the links inside the site.
On a larger screen, switch between **Desktop** and **Phone** widths to check
the mobile layout.

## Use a template

Select **Use this template**. Keystone creates a new site from the template in
your workspace and opens it in the builder, ready to refine. If you are signed
out, Keystone asks you to sign in first, then brings you back to the template.

From there, refine it like any other site. Good first requests:

- "Change the business name to Juniper Bakery and rewrite the copy for a bakery in Austin"
- "Use a deep green and cream palette instead"
- "Remove the pricing page and add an FAQ to the home page"

A template is a starting point, not a layout you are locked into: every page,
color, and section can change in chat.

<Callout type="info">
  A site started from a template takes one of your workspace's sites, like any
  other site. At your plan's [site limit](/products/keystone/workspaces#sites),
  **Use this template** explains the limit instead of creating a site; keep
  building on an existing site or upgrade the plan.
</Callout>

## Link to a template

Every template has a lowercase id, and `/templates?preview=<id>` opens the
gallery with that template's preview showing. For example,
[keystone.omni.dev/templates?preview=wedding](https://keystone.omni.dev/templates?preview=wedding)
opens the Wedding template. Add `?category=<category>` to open the gallery
filtered, for example `/templates?category=events`.

Templates draw their artwork with code (shapes, gradients, and illustrations)
rather than photos, so nothing breaks or needs licensing. To use your own
photos, give Keystone their web addresses and say where they go.

  The forms in a template (newsletter, contact, RSVP) are placeholders until you

  to show your Halo store's products. See

</Callout>
