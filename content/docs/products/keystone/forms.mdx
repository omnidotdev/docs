---
title: Forms
description: Collect contact, booking, and RSVP requests from your visitors in each site's inbox
---

Forms on a Keystone site send what your visitors enter to that site's inbox in
Keystone. Contact, enquiry, booking, RSVP, registration, volunteer, and
newsletter forms all work this way, with no keys, accounts, or setup.

## Add a form

Ask for a form in chat, and say what it should collect, for example:

> Add a contact form with name, email, and message

> Add an RSVP form that asks for each guest's name and meal choice

You can also select the **Add a contact form** suggestion under the chat (see
[Building in Chat](/products/keystone/building)). Keystone builds the form to
match your design, with a label on every field and a confirmation message once
it is sent.

Templates that include a form (contact, enquiry, reservation, RSVP,
registration, volunteer, or newsletter) are ready to collect submissions as
soon as you publish. A form on a site you built before forms worked sends
nowhere: ask Keystone to make it a working form, or select **Add a contact
form**.

## Where forms work

| Where | What a visitor sees |
|-------|---------------------|
| [Hosted site](/products/keystone/publishing#host-on-fractal) | The form sends, on every page |
| [Preview link](/products/keystone/publishing#preview-link) | The form sends (a preview link shows only the home page) |
| Builder and template previews | Nothing is sent. The form says **Submissions arrive once your site is published.** |

A site hosted on Fractal before forms were available may need one republish
before its forms can send. Select **Republish on Fractal** in the builder.

## Read submissions

Each published site has its own inbox. Open it from:

- **Submissions** on the site's card in **Your sites**, which also shows how
  many submissions are unread
- The inbox icon in the builder's header, once the site is published

Submissions are listed newest first, each with the form it came from, when it
arrived, and every answer. Unread submissions are highlighted. You can:

- Select **Mark read** or **Mark unread** on a submission, or **Mark all read**
- Select **Delete** to remove a submission. Keystone asks you to confirm, and a
  deleted submission cannot be recovered
- Select an email address in a submission to reply from your own email

Only members of the site's [workspace](/products/keystone/workspaces) can open
its inbox.

## Email notifications

New submissions can also be emailed to you. The first member to publish a site
gets its notifications at their own email address. To change this, open the
site's inbox:

- **Send them to me** sends notifications to your address instead
- **Turn off** stops them
- **Email me new submissions** turns them back on, to your address

To keep a busy or spammed form from flooding your email, Keystone sends at most
one email per site per hour. It says how many new submissions arrived and links
to the inbox. It never includes what a visitor wrote, so read submissions in the
inbox.

If a notification email fails to send, Keystone tries again a few times over the
next hour, then waits until new submissions arrive. Submissions always reach the
inbox, whether or not the email does.

## Limits and spam protection

- Each form carries a hidden field that people never see. Bots that fill it in
  are told their submission was sent, and it is dropped
- One visitor can send a site up to 5 forms in 10 minutes, and a site accepts
  up to 60 submissions an hour
- A submission can have up to 30 fields, 5,000 characters per answer, and
  16,000 characters in all. A visitor who goes over is asked to shorten it.
  File uploads are not sent
- An inbox holds up to 5,000 submissions. When it is full, the site's forms stop
  accepting new ones until you delete some

## Privacy

Omni stores your visitors' submissions on your behalf: you decide what your
forms ask and what you do with the answers, so tell your visitors how you will
use them. Submissions are kept until you delete them, and deleting a site
deletes its submissions. See the [Privacy Policy](https://omni.dev/legal/privacy)
and [Terms of Service](https://omni.dev/legal/terms).
