Keystone sites can include working pieces of the Omni ecosystem instead of
placeholder HTML. You add them in chat: give Keystone the identifier it needs (a
store domain, a creator handle, a repo), and it places a block styled to match
your design. Blocks are wired up when you publish, so they show live data on both
preview links and hosted sites.

Keystone only adds a block when you supply the matching identifier. Without one,
it builds tasteful placeholder content and tells you what to connect.

| Block | Product | Status |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Product grid and buy buttons | [Halo](/products/halo) | Available |
| Blog post list | [Halo](/products/halo) | Available |
| Support button | [Crystal](/products/crystal) | Available (opens your Crystal page) |
| Repo showcase | [Arbor](/products/arbor) | Available (link card) |
| Email capture | [Herald](/products/herald) | Coming soon |

## Halo store blocks

Connect a [Halo](/products/halo) store by giving Keystone your store's domain,
the address your storefront is served from, for example:

> Add a shop section that shows my products from shop.example.com

### Product grid

A product grid lists the store's live products, each with its image, title,
description, price (including a crossed-out compare-at price on sale items), and
a **Buy now** button. Products and prices come straight from Halo, so the grid
stays current as you edit your catalog, with no republish needed. If the store
has no products yet, the grid says new products are on the way.

### Buy buttons

Selecting **Buy now** (or a single buy button for one product) starts a checkout
for that product. After checkout, the buyer returns to your store's own domain. If
a checkout cannot be started, the button sends the buyer to your store instead.

### Blog post list

A post list shows the latest published posts from your Halo store, each with its
title, date, excerpt, and a **Read more** toggle with the full text. Ask for it in
chat, for example "add a blog section with my latest three posts from
shop.example.com".

## Crystal support button

Give Keystone your [Crystal](/products/crystal) creator handle to add a support
button, for example "add a Support me button for my Crystal handle janedoe".
Selecting it opens your Crystal page, where visitors can back you.

## Arbor repo showcase

For developer, open-source, and portfolio sites, give Keystone an
[Arbor](/products/arbor) repository as `owner/repo` and it adds a styled card that
links to the repository on Arbor.
