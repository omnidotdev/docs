Select **Publish** in the builder to put your site on the web. You choose how:

| | Preview link | Hosted on Fractal |
|---|---|---|
| **Who can** | Anyone | A signed-in member of the site's workspace |
| **Paid by** | Free | Your workspace's [Fractal plan](https://fractal.omni.dev/pricing), only for sites you host |
| **Address** | A read-only link served by Keystone | Its own address under `fractaldeploy.app` |
| **Pages** | The home page | Every page, with clean URLs (`/about`) |
| **Lives in** | Keystone | Your workspace's Fractal project, with its code in a repository your workspace owns |
| **Custom domain** | No | Yes, within your Fractal plan |
| **Keystone badge** | Shown on Free, removed on Pro and Team | Shown on Free, removed on Pro and Team |
| **Integration blocks** | Live | Live |

Publishing never fails for lack of hosting: if a site cannot be hosted, it still
gets a preview link.

## Preview link

A preview link is a free, read-only view of your site's home page that anyone
with the link can open. On the Free plan it carries a small **Made with
Keystone** badge in the corner. The link always shows the latest version of your
site, so changes you make after publishing appear there without publishing
again.

## Host on Fractal

Select **Host on Fractal** to deploy your site as a live site on
[Fractal](/products/fractal), Omni's cloud. The site belongs to your
**workspace**, not to Keystone:

- **Fractal project**: the site is a static site in your workspace's own Fractal
  project, **Keystone sites**, created the first time anyone in the workspace
  hosts a site. Every member of the workspace sees it in the
  [Fractal dashboard](https://fractal.omni.dev), with Fractal's usual roles, and
  can open its logs, domains, and settings there. After publishing, select
  **Manage in Fractal** to go straight to it.
- **Code repository**: the site's code lives in a repository your workspace owns
  on [Arbor](/products/arbor), Omni's code host, so your team can find and clone
  it. Keystone can update only that one repository, and a workspace admin can
  remove its access. Arbor is in private beta, so members need Arbor access to
  open the repository; to keep a copy on GitHub instead, see
  [Sync to GitHub](#sync-to-github).
- **Your permissions**: Keystone deploys only while you publish, acting with your
  own access in that workspace, and only on the Keystone sites project.

Each hosted site runs in isolation on its own address under `fractaldeploy.app`,
and every page is served (the home page at `/`, other pages at `/<page>`).
Hosted sites **scale to zero** when idle, so an unvisited site uses no compute;
the first visit after a quiet spell can take a moment while it wakes up.

After further changes, select **Republish on Fractal** to update the same site at
the same address. Republishing keeps any custom domain you attached.

If a hosted deploy cannot run for another reason, Keystone gives you a preview
link so you always leave with a working URL. Try hosting again later.

### Hosting and your Fractal plan

Hosting is billed by your workspace's Fractal plan, not by Keystone, and only
for the sites your workspace hosts. Your Keystone plan covers building (credits,
sites, seats); your Fractal plan covers hosting. Fractal's own limits decide
what the workspace can host, such as how many projects and services it can run
and how many custom domains it can use; see
[Fractal plans](https://fractal.omni.dev/pricing).

If the workspace's Fractal plan has no room for another site, the publish
dialog says so, gives you the preview link, and links to **Upgrade on Fractal**,
which upgrades that workspace's Fractal plan on your Omni account and brings you
back to the site. If you cannot deploy in the workspace on Fractal at all, ask a
workspace owner for access.

Changing your Keystone plan never takes a hosted site down. To stop hosting a
site, delete its service in the Fractal dashboard.

## Choose a workspace

If you belong to more than one workspace, the publish dialog asks which one to
publish under. It starts on the workspace the site already belongs to. Pick
another workspace to move the site into it; that workspace must have room under
its Keystone site limit and a seat for each of its members. A hosted site moves
its hosting too: its service is removed from the old workspace's Fractal project
and created in the new one, and the new workspace's Fractal plan applies. The
old repository stays with the workspace that owns it. See
[Workspaces and Sites](/products/keystone/workspaces).

## Custom domains

For a site hosted on Fractal, enter a domain such as `www.example.com` in the
**Custom domain** field before you select **Host on Fractal**.

1. Enter the domain and select **Host on Fractal** (or **Republish on Fractal**).
2. Keystone shows the DNS records to add, as a table of **Type**, **Name**, and
   **Value**.
3. Add those records at your domain registrar or DNS provider.
4. Once DNS has propagated, your site is served on your domain, with HTTPS handled
   for you.

Custom domains count toward your workspace's Fractal plan; the site's own
`fractaldeploy.app` address does not. To add more custom domains than the plan
includes, upgrade the workspace's Fractal plan.

## Sync to GitHub

To keep a copy of your site's code in your own GitHub repository, link the site
to a repository from the publish dialog. See [GitHub Sync](/products/keystone/github-sync).
