**Keystone** is Omni's AI website builder. Describe the site you want, and Keystone designs and writes it, shows it in a live preview, and refines it turn by turn as you chat. When it looks right, publish it as a shareable preview link; hosted sites with your own domain are coming soon on paid plans.

Keystone sites are not static mockups. A store page can list your real Halo products with working buy buttons, a blog page can show your latest Halo posts, and a sign-up form can add subscribers to a Herald audience.

## Key Features

- **Describe it, do not build it**, write what you want in plain language and refine the result one message at a time
- **Template gallery**, 12 ready-made, multi-page sites (shops, portfolios, events, software, and more) to start from, previewed live, plus 16 briefs for describing a site from scratch
- **Model choice**, pick the AI model for each turn; generation spends AI credits
- **Live preview**, desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes, page tabs, one-tap follow-up suggestions, and a code download
- **Publishing**, a read-only preview link, or a hosted site in your workspace's own Fractal project with your own domain, billed by your Fractal plan
- **GitHub sync**, keep a copy of your site's code in your own GitHub repository, pushed on every publish
- **Ecosystem blocks**, live Halo product grids, buy buttons, and blog posts, Herald sign-up forms, plus Crystal support buttons and Arbor repo cards
