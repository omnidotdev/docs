**Keystone** is Omni's AI website builder. Describe the site you want, and Keystone designs and writes it, shows it in a live preview, and refines it turn by turn as you chat. When it looks right, publish it: share a free preview link, or, on a paid plan, deploy it as a live hosted site with your own domain.

Keystone sites are not static mockups. A store page can list your real Halo products with working buy buttons, a blog page can show your latest Halo posts, and a support button can send visitors to your Crystal page.

## Key Features

- **Describe it, do not build it**, write what you want in plain language and refine the result one message at a time
- **Starter gallery**, 16 ready-made briefs (shops, portfolios, blogs, events, and more) to start from
- **Live preview**, desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes, page tabs, one-tap follow-up suggestions, and a code download
- **Model choice**, pick the AI model for each turn; generation spends your workspace's Omni credits
- **Publishing**, a free read-only preview link, or a live hosted site on Fractal with custom domains on paid plans
- **Ecosystem blocks**, live Halo product grids, buy buttons, and blog posts, plus Crystal support buttons and Arbor repo cards
