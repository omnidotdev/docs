This guide takes you from a new account to a published Keystone site.

<Callout type="warn">
  Keystone is **coming soon**. Flows and labels can change before launch.
</Callout>

1. **Sign in with your Omni account**
   Open [keystone.omni.dev](https://keystone.omni.dev) and select **Start
   building**. Building requires a signed-in Omni account with a verified email
   address, which keeps generation fair and your sites saved. If you do not have
   an account yet, create one when prompted. A prompt you typed on the home page
   before signing in carries over to the builder.
2. **Check your workspace**
   Keystone builds in your personal Omni workspace: new sites are saved there,
   and generation spends its Omni credits. See
   [Workspaces and Sites](/products/keystone/workspaces).
3. **Describe your site**
   In the chat panel, describe the site in plain language: what it is for, the
   sections you want, and the look and feel. Or pick a
   [starter](/products/keystone/starters) to fill the prompt with a ready-made
   brief. Nothing generates until you send.
4. **Send and watch it build**
   Select the send button (or press **⌘ Enter** / **Ctrl Enter**). Keystone
   designs and writes the site, and the [live preview](/products/keystone/preview)
   shows it as soon as the turn finishes.
5. **Refine it turn by turn**
   Ask for changes the way you would ask a designer: "make the hero bolder", "add
   a pricing section", "add an About page". Each message revises the current site.
   See [Building in Chat](/products/keystone/building).
6. **Publish**
   Select **Publish** to share a free read-only preview link, or, on a paid
   plan, deploy a live hosted site with an optional custom domain. See
   [Publishing](/products/keystone/publishing).

## What a site is made of

A Keystone site has a shared theme (colors, type, and spacing), a shared header
and footer, and one or more pages. Every turn can change any of these, so a
request like "switch to a dark theme" restyles every page at once, and "add a
Menu page linked from the navigation" adds a page and updates the shared header.

## Where your work is saved

Your site, its pages, and the chat transcript are saved as you go. How many
sites you can keep, and which site the builder opens, depends on your plan's
site limit. See [Workspaces and Sites](/products/keystone/workspaces#sites).
