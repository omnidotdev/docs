---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Common Keystone messages and what to do about them
---

| Message or symptom | What it means | What to do |
|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| **Sign in to start building** | Building needs an Omni account | Sign in, or create an account |
| **Verify your email to start building** | Your account's email address is not verified yet | Verify your email from your Omni account, then reload the builder |
| **Generation needs a workspace** | Your account is not in a workspace yet | Create or join a workspace, then try again |
| **This workspace is out of Omni credits** | The workspace's credit balance is used up | Select **Buy credits**, or wait for your monthly allowance. See [Plans and Credits](/products/keystone/plans) |
| **Too many generation requests, please slow down** | You sent prompts faster than the fair-use limit | Wait a moment, then send again |
| **Generation is temporarily at capacity** | Free generation is paused for now to protect capacity | Try again later, or upgrade the workspace to a paid plan |
| **AI generation is temporarily unavailable** | Generation could not run just now | Wait a minute and send again |
| **Picking up where you left off** | The workspace is at its plan's site limit, so the builder reopened your latest site | Keep building on it, or upgrade for more sites |
| **Design-system mode is part of Pro** | Attaching a design system needs a paid plan | Upgrade the workspace, or keep building without one |
| Publishing gave a preview link instead of a hosted site | The workspace you published under has no hosted publishing, or the hosted deploy could not run | Upgrade the workspace to Pro or Team, or publish again later |
| A custom domain was not attached | The workspace's plan does not include custom domains | Upgrade to Pro or Team and republish |
| A hosted site is slow on the first visit | The site scaled to zero while idle and is waking up | Wait a moment; later visits are fast |
| A product grid says the shop is unavailable | The Halo store could not be reached, or the store domain is wrong | Check the store domain in your prompt and that the store is live |
| Integration blocks look empty in the builder | Blocks load live data only once published | Publish, then open the preview link or hosted site |
