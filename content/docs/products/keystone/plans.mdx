Keystone plans belong to a workspace, not to an individual. Each workspace's
plan sets its own site limit and seats. A site's credits, hosting, and custom
domain come from the workspace it belongs to: your personal workspace by
default, or a team workspace you build it in or publish it into.
See [Workspaces and Sites](/products/keystone/workspaces).

## Free

AI web builder for personal sites. **$0**.

- Freeform AI site generation
- Multi-page sites
- Live preview and multi-turn editing
- Shareable read-only preview link
- "Made with Keystone" badge
- Community support

## Pro

Custom domains, design-system mode, and more credits. **$19/month** (or $190/year).

- Everything in Free
- Hosted publishing (dedicated deploy, scales to zero) (coming soon)
- Design-system injection (bring your own tokens and components) (coming soon)
- Custom domains (coming soon)
- Badge removed (with hosted publishing, coming soon)
- Image uploads (coming soon)
- Bring your own LLM key (BYOK) (coming soon)

## Team

Shared workspaces and component manifests for teams. **$49/month** (or $490/year).

- Everything in Pro
- Workspace with multiple seats
- Shared component manifests (coming soon)
- Priority generation (coming soon)
- Higher included credits

<Callout type="info">
  Design-system injection and shared component manifests are becoming
  [Brand kit](/products/keystone/coming-soon#brand-kit), which is coming soon.
</Callout>

## Limits by plan

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sites | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Included credits per month | 150 | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| Hosted publishing | No (preview link) | Coming soon | Coming soon |
| Custom domains | None | Coming soon | Coming soon |
| Seats | 1 | 1 | 10 |

Seats are the number of members a workspace can have and still build with
Keystone. See [Seats](/products/keystone/workspaces#seats).

## Credits

Generation spends Omni credits, and each plan includes a monthly allowance (see
the table above). When the balance reaches zero, generation stops until you buy
more credits or the allowance renews; there is no automatic overage charge on
any plan. See [Credits](/products/keystone/credits).

## Change your plan

Pro and Team open for purchase together with hosted publishing, which is coming
soon; until then the [pricing page](https://keystone.omni.dev/pricing) lists
them as coming soon. Once they open, choose the workspace to upgrade on the
pricing page and pick a plan. To manage an existing subscription, open the
workspace's [Billing page](/products/keystone/workspaces#workspace-billing) and
select **Manage subscription**. Monthly and yearly billing are both available; yearly
costs the same as ten months.

If a workspace's plan ends or moves to one without hosting, its hosted sites
stay live for a 7-day grace period and then go back to their preview links. See
[When a plan stops including hosting](/products/keystone/publishing#when-a-plan-stops-including-hosting).
