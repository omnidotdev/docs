Keystone plans belong to a workspace, not to an individual. Each workspace's
plan sets its own site limit, credits, and seats, and they come from the
workspace a site belongs to: your personal workspace by default, or a team
workspace you build it in or publish it into.
See [Workspaces and Sites](/products/keystone/workspaces).

Keystone plans cover building. Hosting is not part of a Keystone plan: a site
you host runs in your workspace's own Fractal project and is billed by the
workspace's [Fractal plan](https://fractal.omni.dev/pricing), only for the sites
it hosts. See [Hosting and your Fractal plan](/products/keystone/publishing#hosting-and-your-fractal-plan).

## Free

AI web builder for personal sites. **$0**.

- Freeform AI site generation with Omni credits
- Multi-page sites
- Live preview and multi-turn editing
- Shareable read-only preview link
- Host on Fractal in your workspace (billed by your Fractal plan)
- "Made with Keystone" badge
- Community support

## Pro

More sites, more credits, and no Keystone badge. **$19/month** (or $190/year).

- Everything in Free
- Up to 10 sites
- Design-system injection (bring your own tokens and components) (coming soon)
- Badge removed
- Image uploads (coming soon)
- Bring your own LLM key (BYOK) (coming soon)

## Team

Shared workspaces and component manifests for teams. **$49/month** (or $490/year).

- Everything in Pro
- Workspace with multiple seats
- Shared component manifests (coming soon)
- Priority generation (coming soon)
- Higher included credits

<Callout type="info">
  Design-system injection and shared component manifests are becoming
  [Brand kit](/products/keystone/coming-soon#brand-kit), which is coming soon.
</Callout>

## Limits by plan

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sites | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Included credits per month | None | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| Keystone badge | Shown | Removed | Removed |
| Seats | 1 | 1 | 10 |

Seats are the number of members a workspace can have and still build with
Keystone. See [Seats](/products/keystone/workspaces#seats).

## Credits

Generation spends [AI credits](/help/ai-credits). Pro and Team include a
monthly allowance (see the table above) that does not roll over; Free includes
no credits, so generating on Free uses credits the workspace has bought. When
the balance reaches zero, generation stops until you buy more credits or a paid
plan's allowance renews; there is no automatic overage charge on any plan. See
[Credits](/products/keystone/credits).

## Change your plan

Pro and Team open for purchase when Keystone launches; until then the
[pricing page](https://keystone.omni.dev/pricing) lists them as coming soon.
Once they open, choose the workspace to upgrade on the
pricing page and pick a plan. To manage an existing subscription, open the
workspace's [Billing page](/products/keystone/workspaces#workspace-billing) and
select **Manage subscription**. Monthly and yearly billing are both available; yearly
costs the same as ten months.

Changing a workspace's Keystone plan never takes a hosted site down: hosting
follows the workspace's Fractal plan.
