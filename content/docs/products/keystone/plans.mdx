Keystone plans belong to a workspace, not to an individual. Every member of the
workspace builds under its plan and spends its credits.

## Free

AI web builder for personal sites, self-hostable. **$0**.

- Freeform AI site generation
- Live preview and multi-turn editing
- Shareable read-only preview link
- "Made with Keystone" badge
- Self-hostable (Apache-2.0)
- Community support

## Pro

Custom domains, design-system mode, and more credits. **$19/month** (or $190/year).

- Everything in Free
- Hosted publishing (dedicated deploy, scales to zero)
- Design-system injection (bring your own tokens and components)
- Custom domains
- Badge removed
- Multi-page sites and media
- Bring your own LLM key (BYOK) (coming soon)

## Team

Shared workspaces and component manifests for teams. **$49/month** (or $490/year).

- Everything in Pro
- Workspace with multiple seats
- Shared component manifests
- Priority generation (coming soon)
- Higher included credits

## How credits work

Generation spends **Omni credits**, a single balance per workspace that is shared
across every Omni product. Each plan adds its included credits every month.

- A turn's cost depends on the model you pick and the size of your site. See
  [Building in Chat](/products/keystone/building#credits).
- When the balance reaches zero, generation stops until you buy more credits or
  your monthly allowance renews. There is no automatic overage charge on any plan.
- Your balance shows in the builder header, with a link to buy more.

Browsing starters, previewing, downloading your code, and publishing never spend
credits.

## Change your plan

Open the [pricing page](https://keystone.omni.dev/pricing), choose the workspace
to upgrade, and pick a plan. Monthly and yearly billing are both available; yearly
costs the same as ten months.
