These features are in progress and are **not available yet**. They are described
here so you know what is on the way; details can change before they ship.

## Brand kit

<Callout type="warn">**Coming soon.**</Callout>

A brand kit grounds every generation in your brand, so new pages and revisions
match your colors, typography, and spacing instead of starting from a generic
look. Brand kits are powered by [Aura](https://aura.omni.dev), Omni's design-token
tool: design your tokens there (in the W3C design tokens format), then attach them
to a site.

Brand kit is the new name for the design-system feature listed on the Pro and
Team plans as "Design-system injection" and "Shared component manifests". See
[Plans and Credits](/products/keystone/plans).

## Point-and-edit

<Callout type="warn">**Coming soon.**</Callout>

Hover any part of your site in the preview, click it, and change its color, font,
spacing, or text directly. Point-and-edit is for quick tweaks: no prompt to
write, and no credits spent.

## Arbor repos

<Callout type="warn">**Coming soon.**</Callout>

Keep your site's code in a repository on [Arbor](/products/arbor), Omni's code
host, as an alternative to [GitHub repo sync](/products/keystone/publishing#sync-to-github).
