A starter is a ready-made brief for a common kind of site. Picking one fills the
prompt with a detailed description you can read and edit before sending. Nothing
generates, and no credits are spent, until you send the prompt.

## Browse the gallery

The starter gallery appears on the [Keystone home page](https://keystone.omni.dev)
and in the builder's preview panel before your first message. Filter it by
category: **All**, **Business**, **Store**, **Portfolio**, **Blog**, **Events**,
or **Personal**. Each card shows a sketch of the kind of site the starter
produces.

The builder's chat panel also offers three quick picks (Coffee roaster, Product
launch, and Photographer) with a link to browse the full gallery.

## Customize before you send

A starter is a starting point, not a template you are locked into. Edit the
filled-in prompt to swap in your business name, products, colors, or sections,
then send it. After the first turn, keep refining in chat like any other site.

## Link to a starter

Every starter has a lowercase slug, and `/build?starter=<slug>` opens the
builder with that starter's brief preloaded into a blank site. For example,
[keystone.omni.dev/build?starter=wedding](https://keystone.omni.dev/build?starter=wedding)
opens the builder with the Wedding brief ready to review.
