title: Starters

A starter is a ready-made brief for a common kind of site, for when you would
rather describe a site from scratch than start from a finished
[template](/products/keystone/templates). Picking one fills the prompt with a
detailed description you can read and edit before sending. Nothing generates,
and no credits are spent, until you send the prompt.

## Pick a starter

Before your first message, the builder's chat panel offers three quick picks
(Online boutique, Local service, and Podcast), and any starter opens from a
link (see below). The builder's preview panel shows the
[template gallery](/products/keystone/templates) instead, for starting from a
finished site.

## Customize before you send

A starter is a starting point, not a layout you are locked into. Edit the
filled-in prompt to swap in your business name, products, colors, or sections,
then send it. After the first turn, keep refining in chat like any other site.

## Link to a starter

Every starter has a lowercase slug, and `/build?starter=<slug>` opens the
builder with that starter's brief filled into the prompt. For example,
[keystone.omni.dev/build?starter=wedding](https://keystone.omni.dev/build?starter=wedding)
opens the builder with the Wedding brief ready to review.

The brief only fills the prompt; nothing is sent. If you are at your plan's
[site limit](/products/keystone/workspaces#sites), the builder reopens your most
recent site, and sending the brief revises that site rather than starting a new
one.

| Product launch | `product-launch` | Business | Software launch page with features and pricing tiers |
| Local service | `local-service` | Business | Trustworthy page for a trade or home service |
| Nonprofit | `nonprofit` | Business | Mission, impact, and a donate button |
| Photographer | `photographer` | Portfolio | Dark, image-first portfolio with galleries |
| Design studio | `design-studio` | Portfolio | Bold agency site with case studies |
| Developer portfolio | `developer` | Portfolio | Projects, open-source work, and writing |
| Personal blog | `personal-blog` | Blog | Readable writing home with posts and a newsletter |
| Recipe blog | `food-blog` | Blog | Recipes, categories, and a cookbook promo |
| Conference | `conference` | Events | Event site with speakers, schedule, and tickets |
| Wedding | `wedding` | Events | Our story, the day, travel, and RSVP |

| Online resume | `resume` | Personal | A polished one-page CV |
| Podcast | `podcast` | Personal | Episodes, hosts, and subscribe links |
