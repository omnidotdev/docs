Facet can turn a plain-English description into a working model. Open the
**Describe a part** panel in the Studio, type what you want, and Facet writes
the Facet code, loads it into the editor, and runs it. The result is ordinary
code: read it, edit it, add parameters, and iterate like any other model.

<Callout type="info">
AI generation is in **Beta**. It writes the same API you would write by hand
(`cube`, `cylinder`, `union`, `subtract`, `fillet`, and the rest), so treat its
output as a starting point you refine, not a finished part.
</Callout>

## Generate a model

1. Open the Studio at [facet.omni.dev](https://facet.omni.dev).
2. In the **Describe a part** panel, enter a prompt. Be specific about
   dimensions and units (Facet works in millimeters), for example:
   `a 60 by 40 by 20 mm enclosure with 2 mm walls and four 3 mm mounting holes`.
3. Select **Generate**. Facet writes the model into the editor, runs it, and
   shows the result in the viewport.

If the generated model fails to build, Facet automatically sends the error back
and tries to repair it a couple of times before showing you a message. Your
previous model is kept if generation does not produce a working result.

## Access and credits

Local modeling and export never require an account and always run on your
device. AI generation has two paths:

- **Bring your own key.** Expand **Use your own key** and paste an AI model
  provider API key. The key is stored only in your browser and is sent with each
  request so it reaches the provider directly. Generation with your own key needs
  no account and is billed to your provider, not metered against Facet credits.
- **Managed generation** (rolling out). Sign in with your Omni account to
  generate without supplying a key. Managed generation is metered in credits (one
  credit is about one US cent of model spend): the free tier includes a fixed
  monthly allowance and is hard-capped, and paid tiers include more. During the
  Beta, managed generation is still rolling out, so bring-your-own-key is the way
  to generate today.

<Callout type="warn">
Bring-your-own-key is the no-account option and the way to generate during the
Beta. Expand **Use your own key** in the panel and paste your provider key.
</Callout>

## Your data

When you generate, the prompt you enter (and any code resubmitted to refine or
repair a result) is sent to Facet's AI gateway and routed through the Synapse AI
router to a third-party AI model provider, which processes it to return a result.
Prompts and generated output are not stored on Facet's servers; only a per-user
monthly credit count is kept, to enforce plan limits. Local modeling and file
export send nothing off your device unless you use AI generation. See the
[Privacy Policy](https://omni.dev/legal/privacy) for details.

## Tips

- Name real dimensions and units; vague prompts produce vague parts.
- Ask for exact-geometry operations by name (fillet, chamfer, shell) when you
  want them; the Studio switches to the B-rep kernel automatically.
- Generate a rough first version, then wire up `param.number(...)` sliders and
  adjust the code by hand for a precise, reusable model.
