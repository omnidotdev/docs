---
title: Leveling
description: Reward activity with XP, ranks, a leaderboard, and level roles
---

Charm awards experience points to members for chatting, tracks their level and
rank, and can grant roles when members reach a level.

The `/levels` configuration commands require the **Manage Server** permission.
`/rank` and `/leaderboard` are available to everyone.

## Enable leveling

```
/levels enable true
```

By default, Charm announces a level-up in the channel where the member was
chatting. To send every announcement to one channel instead:

```
/levels channel #level-ups
```

Run `/levels channel` with no channel to go back to announcing in the member's
current channel.

## Level-reward roles

Grant a role automatically when members reach a level:

```
/levels role add level:10 role:@Regular
```

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/levels role add <level> <role>` | Grant this role when members reach the level |
| `/levels role remove <level>` | Remove the reward at that level |
| `/levels role list` | List the level-reward roles |

Granting level roles uses the **Manage Roles** permission, so Charm's own role
must sit above the reward roles in the role list.

## Ranks and leaderboard

- `/rank [user]` shows your rank, level, total XP, and progress to the next level,
  or another member's when you name one.
- `/leaderboard` shows the server's top members by XP.
