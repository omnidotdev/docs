title: Getting Started

---

Runa runs in the browser. There is nothing to install. Create an account, set up
a workspace, and you have a working board in a couple of minutes.

<Callout type="info">
  Want to look around first? Open the [interactive demo](https://runa.omni.dev/demo)
  to explore a board without signing up.
</Callout>

## 1. Create an account

Go to [runa.omni.dev](https://runa.omni.dev) and sign in. Runa uses your Omni
identity, so the same account works across every Omni product.

## 2. Create a workspace

A **workspace** is the home for your team's projects and members. On first sign-in
you are prompted to create one. Name it for your team, company, or just yourself.

You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the workspace
switcher in the sidebar.

## 3. Set up a project

Inside the workspace, create a **project** for a body of work (a product launch, a
client engagement, a personal goal). A new project starts with a default set of
**columns** (the stages work moves through, for example To Do, In Progress, Done).

Rename columns, add your own, and set their order to match how your team actually
works. One column is marked the **done column**; tasks there count as complete.

## 4. Add a task

Create a **task** for a single piece of work. Give it a title, and optionally a
description, a priority, a due date, labels, and assignees. The task appears as a
card in its column.

Drag the card between columns as the work progresses, or reorder cards within a
column to set priority.

## 5. Invite your team

Open **Workspace Settings → Members** to invite people by email. Each member joins
with a role (Owner, Admin, or Member) that governs what they can change. See
[Permissions](/docs/products/runa/permissions) for the full breakdown.

## 6. Choose how you work

Switch between views from the project header:

- **Board** for visual, drag-and-drop workflow
- **List** for quick scanning and keyboard-driven work
- **Calendar** for anything with a due date, including a cross-project view of your
  own tasks

Next, read [Concepts](/docs/products/runa/concepts) for how Runa models your work,
or jump to [Tasks](/docs/products/runa/tasks) for everything a task can hold.
