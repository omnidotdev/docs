---
title: Notifications
description: Email notifications for task activity, and how to choose which ones you get.
---

Runa emails you about activity on tasks you're involved in. A task you're involved in is one you created, are assigned to, or have commented on.

| Email | Sent to |
|-------|---------|
| **Assigned to a task** | The person assigned |
| **New comment** | Everyone involved in the task, except the commenter |
| **@mention** | The person mentioned, in a comment or a task description |
| **Task moved to another project** | The task's creator and assignees |

Runa never emails you about your own actions, and never emails a whole workspace.

## Choose your emails

Open **Settings** in any workspace and use the **Notifications** section to turn each email type on or off. Assignment emails can also be batched into a single digest. These preferences apply across all of your workspaces.
