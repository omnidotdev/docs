title: Tasks
description: Everything a task can hold, from description to activity
---

A **task** is the core unit of work in Runa. It lives in a column on a project
board and carries everything needed to track a single piece of work. Open a task
to see its full detail; on the board and in the calendar it appears as a compact
card.

## Title and description

Every task has a title. The description is a rich text editor that supports
headings, lists, checklists, code, quotes, and links.

- **Checklists** break a task into tickable sub-items.
- **Links** render as clickable previews, showing the linked page's title where it
  is available.
- **@mentions** tag a workspace member in the description or a comment.

## Priority

Set a priority of **low**, **medium**, or **high**. Priority tints the task's card
on the board and the calendar so the most important work stands out at a glance.

## Due dates

Give a task a due date so it appears on the [calendar](/docs/products/runa/calendar).
A due date can be a day alone, or a day with a specific time. Timed tasks surface
at their time; all-day tasks sit at the top of the day.

A task past its due date that is not yet done reads as **overdue** and is flagged
on the calendar.

## Labels

Apply **labels** to categorize tasks within a project. Each label has a name, a
color, and an optional icon, and label colors show as dots on the card. Labels are
defined per project, and a workspace can share a common set across its projects.

## Assignees

Assign one or more workspace members to a task to show who is responsible.
Assignees appear as a stack of avatars on the card, on the board, and on the
calendar. The number of assignees a task can carry depends on your
[plan](/docs/products/runa/permissions).

## Attachments

Attach files to a task (and to comments). Images show an inline preview; other
files show as a download. Attachment size and total storage depend on your plan.

## Comments

Discuss a task in its **comments**. Comments support the same rich text as
descriptions, including @mentions and attachments, and members can react to a
comment with an emoji.

## Activity

Each task keeps an **activity** feed: a running, most-recent-first record of what
happened, including creation, field changes, assignment, labeling, comments, and
attachments. Consecutive actions of the same kind by the same person collapse into
one row to keep the feed readable.
