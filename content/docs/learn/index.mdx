Running anything online means bumping into the same handful of concepts: domains, DNS, hosting, certificates, email. Most explanations either assume you already know the jargon or bury the one thing that actually matters.

These guides do the opposite. Each one is short, in plain English, and ends where a generic answer stops: a real recommendation and the specific mistakes that bite people, drawn from years of hands-on work rather than a textbook definition.

## How these guides are written

Every guide follows the same shape:

- **What it is**, in a sentence or two, no jargon.
- **What we recommend**, and why, so you are not left to guess.
- **The mistakes that actually cost people**, the ones worth avoiding.
- **How it connects to Omni**, where a product of ours solves the problem.

<Callout type="info">
  These are general educational guides, not advice tailored to your specific
  situation. For anything high-stakes, treat them as a solid starting point and
  confirm the details against your own setup.
</Callout>
