Arbor is open core: the platform is free and open source (Apache 2.0) to self-host, and the hosted service at [arbor.omni.dev](https://arbor.omni.dev) adds paid plans that unlock deeper graph capabilities. Entitlements are enforced at the API by Aether and surfaced through the app.

Pricing is **per organization**, billed as a flat rate for the whole organization rather than per seat. Yearly billing is 25% off the monthly rate (nine months for the year).

## Plans

Pricing and tier definitions are owned by the [omni-api product catalog](https://github.com/omnidotdev/omni-api/blob/master/services/api/src/lib/db/catalog/planConfigs.ts) under the `arbor` key. The values below mirror that source of truth.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Graph | Collaborators | Storage | Support |
|------|---------|--------|-------|---------------|---------|---------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Per-repository | Up to 5 per repository | 1 GB | Community |
| Pro | $10 / org / mo | $90 / org / yr | Org-wide polyrepo | Unlimited | 25 GB | Email |
| Team | $25 / org / mo | $225 / org / yr | Blast-radius + orchestration | Unlimited | 100 GB | Priority |

### Free

- Unlimited public and private repositories
- Per-repository dependency graph
- Up to 5 collaborators per repository
- 1 GB storage
- Community support

### Pro

- Everything in Free
- Org-wide polyrepo graph
- [Weaver](/armory/weaver) dependency detection
- Advanced graph visualization
- Unlimited collaborators
- 25 GB storage
- Email support

### Team

- Everything in Pro
- Dependency blast-radius analysis
- Multi-repo [Weaver](/armory/weaver) orchestration
- 100 GB storage
- Priority support

## The Graph Capability Ladder

Every plan includes the graph, but what the graph can see and reason about scales with your plan. This is the core of what each paid tier unlocks:

- **Free (per-repository graph)**: each repository gets its own dependency graph. You see the structure inside a single repository, but repositories remain separate islands.
- **Pro (org-wide polyrepo graph)**: the graph spans your entire organization. [Weaver](/armory/weaver) dependency detection surfaces the edges between repositories automatically, so a polyrepo or microservice architecture renders as one connected topology with advanced visualization.
- **Team (blast-radius and orchestration)**: on top of the org-wide graph, Team adds dependency blast-radius analysis (trace what a change to one repository can affect downstream) and multi-repo Weaver orchestration (drive coordinated operations across the graph).

## Operational Limits

The hosted API enforces these limits per organization. A value of "Unlimited" corresponds to `-1` in the product catalog.

| Limit | Free | Pro | Team |
|-------|------|-----|------|
| `max_private_repos` | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| `max_storage_bytes` | 1 GB | 25 GB | 100 GB |
| `max_collaborators` | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| `graph_level` | Per-repository | Org-wide polyrepo | Blast-radius + orchestration |

## Open Source Projects

Arbor is built on open source and we want maintainers on it. There is no automatic open-source discount applied by billing, so this is a manual program: if you maintain public repositories and want your organization on a paid plan at no cost, [contact us](mailto:support@omni.dev) and we will set it up by hand. Do not expect the upgrade to apply itself, reach out and we will arrange it.

## Self-Hosting

Self-hosters do not pay anything to use Arbor. The Apache 2.0 license covers commercial use, modification, and redistribution. A self-hosted instance runs at free-tier graph capability (per-repository) by default. The paid graph tiers are a hosted-plan capability enforced through Aether, so they apply on [arbor.omni.dev](https://arbor.omni.dev) or on a self-hosted instance that you deliberately wire up to Aether. See [Self-Hosting](/armory/arbor/self-hosting) for setup.

## Billing

Hosted plans are billed per organization via Aether. Invoices, plan changes, and cancellations are handled in the Omni Console. For enterprise terms or dedicated support, [contact us](mailto:support@omni.dev).
